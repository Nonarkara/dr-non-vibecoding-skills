# ch02 — Signals, Signs, and Symbols

> A precise vocabulary for different types of message-bearing entities.

Not all communication works the same way. We must distinguish between three types of links. A 'Signal' is a mechanical trigger that causes an automatic response, like a mother responding to an infant's cry or a light switch turning on a bulb. An 'Index' is a static indicator that points to something else, such as smoke indicating fire. Within indices, we find 'Signa,' which are cultural conventions. A 'Sign' is a type of signum where the relationship is intrinsic because both parts belong to the same context—like a crown being a sign for royalty. A 'Symbol' is a signum where the association is arbitrary and the two parts come from different contexts—like using a lion to represent a king. Understanding these distinctions prevents the error of treating a metaphor as a physical cause.
