# ch10 — Decoding Cultural Systems

> The method for interpreting complex symbolic behavior.

To decode a cultural system, the analyst must look for the structure that remains constant across different modes of expression. A myth told in words might have the same underlying structure as a ritual performed in a temple or the way a house is built. This is called 'transformation.' We look for pairs of opposites—like high/low, left/right, or clean/dirty—and see how they are used to organize different parts of life. By 'adding up' these different metaphors, we can find the core logic of the culture. The goal is to find the 'common abstract reality' that underlies the various performances of social life.
