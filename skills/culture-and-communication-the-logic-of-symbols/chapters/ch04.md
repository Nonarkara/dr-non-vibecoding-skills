# ch04 — The Mental Image

> How we map the external world into internal concepts.

There is a gap between the objects in the world, our sense-images of them, and the concepts in our minds. While we think our perception is natural, it is actually conditioned by our social environment. We use language to cut up the visual continuum into meaningful objects. An animal is just an animal until we name it 'dog' or 'pig.' Once named, the word becomes part of the concept. This allows us to play mental games. We can take the attributes of a 'pig' and apply them to a 'policeman.' This is a metaphoric shift. Because the link between the name and the object is arbitrary, we can use these labels to re-order the world in our heads, creating new meanings that don't exist in nature.
