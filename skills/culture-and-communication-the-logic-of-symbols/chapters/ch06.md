# ch06 — Carving the World

> How culture creates boundaries in space and time.

The physical world is a continuum, but human social life requires clear divisions. We use symbols to create artificial boundaries. In space, we distinguish 'mine' from 'yours' or 'sacred' from 'secular.' In time, we use clocks and calendars to break the flow of life into segments like 'work days' and 'holidays.' These boundaries are not found in nature; they are human inventions. Because boundaries are artificial, they are inherently unstable and a source of anxiety. We mark them with physical barriers like fences or with social barriers like taboos. The area where two categories meet is always 'ambiguous' and is usually treated as 'sacred' or 'dangerous' because it does not fit the standard classification system.
