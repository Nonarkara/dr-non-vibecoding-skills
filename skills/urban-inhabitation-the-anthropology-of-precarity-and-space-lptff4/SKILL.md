## Core principles

- Space is socially produced through daily interactions and power struggles, not just physical architecture.
- Precarity is a strategic modality used by capital to control labor by maintaining long-term instability.
- Urban inhabitants use logistics to transport themselves across differentiated social and physical environments.
- Public spaces are moral arenas where conflicting urban orders are negotiated and contested.
- The city is a resource to be harvested, where waste and fallow spaces are repurposed for survival.
- Detachment is a tactical maneuver to avoid the scrutiny of formal institutions and surveillance.
- Inequality is multidimensional, involving the asymmetric distribution of territory and the power to define belonging.
- Informal labor groups create urban insertion by trespassing symbolic and physical boundaries.
