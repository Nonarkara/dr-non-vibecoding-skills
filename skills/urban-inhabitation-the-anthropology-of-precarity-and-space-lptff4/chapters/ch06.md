# ch06 — Tactical Detachment

> How individuals use invisibility and mobility to avoid surveillance and institutional control.

Detachment is a refusal to be 'pinned down' by the expectations of the state, family, or the formal economy. In a world where every detail of a person's life is tracked and assessed, many youth actively pursue ways to subtract themselves from visibility. This might involve spreading oneself across multiple small projects to hedge bets, or using 'floating' populations to move through the city without leaving a permanent trace. Detachment is often a response to the 'cruel irony' of identity politics; by appearing to belong to a narrow group, an individual can actually hide their true movements. When observing urban sociality, look for 'continuous exit'—the practice of moving through doors that hinge rather than hedge the future.
