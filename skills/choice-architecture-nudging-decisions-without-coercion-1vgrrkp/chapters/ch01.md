# ch01 — Two Minds: Automatic vs. Reflective

> Explains the dual‑system model of human cognition and why most everyday decisions are driven by the fast, unconscious Automatic System.

Human thinking splits into an Automatic System that reacts instantly and a Reflective System that deliberates slowly. The Automatic System handles routine tasks—catching a ball, smiling at a puppy—without conscious effort. The Reflective System steps in for calculations, planning, or when a decision feels important. Because the Automatic System dominates most daily choices, people often accept the first option presented, especially when it aligns with their gut feelings. The Reflective System can override these impulses, but it requires attention and effort that users rarely allocate. Designers must therefore anticipate which system will dominate at each interaction point and shape the environment accordingly.
