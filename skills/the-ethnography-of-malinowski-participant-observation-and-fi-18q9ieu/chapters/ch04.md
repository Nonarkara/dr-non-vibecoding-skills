# ch04 — The Logic of the Sub-Clan

> Matrilineal descent and the hidden power structures that govern land and magic.

While the 'clan' is the most visible social unit, the 'sub-clan' is the true engine of Trobriand law and economics. Descent is matrilineal, meaning every person traces their lineage through their mother to a common ancestress who emerged from the ground. This sub-clan owns the land, the magic, and the rank. Even in a village that appears patriarchal on the surface, the matrilineal law is working behind the scenes. For example, a man's wealth often goes to his sister's children rather than his own. This creates a complex system of obligations where a household is built on a 'two-fold foundation' of kinship and marriage. When researching a community, do not be fooled by the most vocal or visible leaders. Look for the underlying 'kindred' groups that hold the actual rights to resources. Understanding the sub-clan reveals why people make seemingly irrational economic choices; they are often fulfilling a matrilineal obligation that is invisible to the casual observer.
