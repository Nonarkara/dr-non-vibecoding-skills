# Glossary

Terms and named ideas from the source, anchored to the chapter that explains them.

| Term | Definition | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Guya'u** | A full chief or person of the highest rank. | ch07 |
| **Gumguya'u** | A sub-chief or person of secondary rank. | ch07 |
| **Kula** | A ceremonial exchange system of shell valuables across the archipelago. | ch05 |
| **Sub-clan** | A matrilineal kindred group that owns land and magic; the primary unit of law. | ch04 |
| **Matrilineal** | A system where descent and inheritance are traced through the mother's line. | ch04 |
| **Yam-house** | A decorative storehouse for yams, symbolizing wealth and status. | ch02 |
| **Milamala** | The annual feast of the spirits, marking the return of the dead and a period of plenty. | ch08 |
| **Kayasa** | A competitive gardening event or period of organized communal activity. | ch02 |
| **Taytu** | The small yam, the staple food and primary unit of Trobriand wealth. | ch09 |
| **Kuvi** | The large variety of yam, used more for display than daily eating. | ch09 |
| **Malia** | A state of prosperity, fullness, and satiety. | ch08 |
| **Lapi** | The pearl-shell mollusc, a traditional source of food and wealth. | ch03 |
| **Imponderabilia** | The subtle, routine aspects of daily life that cannot be captured by formal questioning. | ch06 |
| **Solicitary Gift** | An initial gift given to provoke or request a service or exchange. | ch05 |
| **Clinching Gift** | The final gift that concludes a transaction or exchange. | ch05 |
| **Tabalu** | The highest-ranking sub-clan in the Trobriand Islands. | ch07 |
| **Kabisawali** | A modern political and economic movement focused on Trobriand self-determination. | ch10 |
| **Participant Observation** | The method of living among a group to study them from the inside. | ch01 |
| **Inward Coherence** | The underlying logic that makes a culture's customs make sense to its members. | ch06 |
| **Damnfool Question** | A naive inquiry into the obvious to uncover fundamental assumptions. | ch06 |
