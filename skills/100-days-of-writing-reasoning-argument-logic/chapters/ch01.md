# ch01 — The Logic of Tradition

> How to evaluate social norms like marriage by looking for their underlying productive logic rather than following them blindly.

Most people follow social traditions because they seem unimaginable to do otherwise. When faced with a norm—such as the expectation to marry by a certain age—you must ask if there is a 'productive logic' behind it. If a tradition is maintained only through social pressure or the fear of being an outcast, it may be a tool of social control rather than a benefit to the individual. Intellectual honesty requires you to admit when you do not understand the purpose of a norm. Instead of conforming to avoid questions, use silence or a standard honest response to maintain your boundaries. The goal is to ensure your life choices are based on your own criteria—such as finding a partner who is intellectual and non-possessive—rather than a desire to fit a pre-made social mold. By questioning the 'unthinkable,' you open up the possibility of alternative ways of living that may be more rational and fulfilling.
