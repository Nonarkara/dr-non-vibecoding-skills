# ch02 — Biopower and Social Stigma

> Understanding how governments use branding and anxiety to manipulate population behavior.

States often use 'biopower'—a term from Michel Foucault—to control populations by managing their bodies and social lives. A prime example is the branding of unmarried women over 27 in China as 'leftover women.' This is not a neutral description but a calculated strategy to fix gender imbalances and promote social stability through the nuclear family. By creating a stigma, the state induces anxiety in citizens, making them feel 'unwanted' even when they are educated, independent, and successful. To resist this, you must recognize that the anxiety you feel is often an external construct. Analyze the source of the branding: who benefits from you feeling like an outcast? When you see that the stigma has no actual underpinning factor other than state utility, the power of that stigma diminishes. Recognizing biopower allows you to separate your self-worth from state-mandated milestones.
