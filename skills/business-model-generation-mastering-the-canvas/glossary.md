# Glossary

Terms and named ideas from the source, anchored to the chapter that explains them.

| Term | Definition | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Business Model Canvas** | A visual template with nine building blocks used to design, describe, and analyze business models. | ch01 |
| **Customer Segment** | A distinct group of people or organizations a business aims to serve and create value for. | ch02 |
| **Value Proposition** | The bundle of products and services that solves a customer problem or satisfies a need for a specific segment. | ch03 |
| **Channel** | A touchpoint used to communicate, deliver, and support the Value Proposition to customers. | ch04 |
| **Customer Relationship** | The type of interaction a business establishes with each segment to acquire, retain, and grow revenue. | ch05 |
| **Revenue Stream** | The cash generated from a Customer Segment, driven by pricing mechanisms and payment types. | ch06 |
| **Key Resource** | An essential asset—physical, financial, intellectual, or human—required to make the business model work. | ch07 |
| **Key Activity** | A critical action a company must perform to operate its business model successfully. | ch08 |
| **Key Partnership** | A cooperative arrangement with suppliers or partners that provides resources, activities, or risk reduction. | ch09 |
| **Cost Structure** | All expenses incurred to operate a business model, split between fixed and variable costs. | ch10 |
| **Multi‑sided Platform** | A business model that serves two or more interdependent Customer Segments that need each other, such as readers and advertisers. | ch02 |
| **Unbundling** | Separating fundamentally different business types—customer relationship, product innovation, and infrastructure—into distinct entities to avoid internal conflicts. | ch02 |
| **Long Tail** | A model that profits from selling a large number of niche items in small quantities, enabled by low distribution costs. | ch02 |
| **FREE** | A business model where core offerings are given at no charge to attract users, with revenue generated from premium services, advertising, or cross‑sales. | ch06 |
| **Open Business Model** | A model that leverages external ideas, technologies, or resources through licensing, partnerships, or crowdsourcing to expand value creation. | ch09 |
| **Mass Market** | A Customer Segment comprising a large group with broadly similar needs and problems. | ch02 |
| **Niche Market** | A specialized Customer Segment with specific, tailored requirements. | ch02 |
| **Diversified Market** | Serving two or more unrelated Customer Segments with different needs using the same business model. | ch02 |
| **Segmented Market** | Serving several Customer Segments with slightly different needs and willingness to pay. | ch02 |
| **Fixed Pricing** | A pricing mechanism where the price is set based on static variables such as list price or product features. | ch06 |
| **Dynamic Pricing** | A pricing mechanism where the price changes according to market conditions such as supply, demand, or time. | ch06 |
| **Subscription Fee** | A recurring Revenue Stream where customers pay regularly for ongoing access to a service. | ch06 |
| **Brokerage Fee** | A Revenue Stream earned by facilitating a transaction between two or more parties and taking a commission. | ch06 |
| **Asset Sale** | A Revenue Stream derived from selling ownership rights to a physical product. | ch06 |
| **Usage Fee** | A Revenue Stream generated by charging customers based on their use of a service. | ch06 |
| **Licensing** | A Revenue Stream created by granting permission to use intellectual property in exchange for a fee. | ch06 |
| **Advertising Revenue** | Income received for exposing a brand, product, or service to an audience. | ch06 |
| **Self‑service** | A Customer Relationship where the company provides tools for customers to help themselves without direct interaction. | ch05 |
| **Dedicated Personal Assistance** | A Customer Relationship where a specific representative is assigned to an individual client. | ch05 |
| **Automated Services** | A Customer Relationship that mixes self‑service with automated processes recognizing individual traits. | ch05 |
| **Community** | An online forum where users help each other and the company gains insights about its customers. | ch05 |
| **Co‑creation** | A Customer Relationship where customers are invited to collaborate in designing or improving products and services. | ch05 |
| **Production Activities** | Key Activities focused on designing, making, and delivering products in quantity or quality. | ch08 |
| **Problem Solving Activities** | Key Activities that involve creating new solutions for individual customer problems. | ch08 |
| **Platform Activities** | Key Activities related to developing, maintaining, and promoting a platform that enables interactions between other parties. | ch08 |
| **Strategic Alliance** | A partnership between non‑competitors to share resources, reduce costs, or access new markets. | ch09 |
| **Coopetition** | A strategic partnership between competitors in some areas while they remain rivals in others. | ch09 |
| **Joint Venture** | A separate legal entity created by two or more parents to pursue a specific business opportunity. | ch09 |
| **Buyer‑Supplier Relationship** | A partnership focused on securing reliable supplies of inputs or distributing outputs. | ch09 |
| **Economies of Scale** | Cost advantages that arise when increasing output lowers the average cost per unit. | ch10 |
| **Economies of Scope** | Cost advantages gained from a broader range of operations, such as sharing marketing across multiple products. | ch10 |
| **Cost‑driven Model** | A business model that prioritizes minimizing expenses wherever possible. | ch10 |
| **Value‑driven Model** | A business model that accepts higher costs to create premium, differentiated value. | ch10 |
