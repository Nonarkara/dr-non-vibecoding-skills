## Core principles

- The rich do not work for money; they acquire or create assets that generate cash flow.
- Financial literacy is the ability to read and understand the relationship between an income statement and a balance sheet.
- An asset is something that puts money into your pocket; a liability is something that takes money out.
- Wealth is measured by the number of days you can survive without working while maintaining your standard of living.
- The middle class works for the government (taxes) and the bank (mortgages) before they work for themselves.
- Financial intelligence is the mental process used to solve financial problems and increase wealth.
- Risk is not the problem; the lack of financial education to manage risk is the problem.
