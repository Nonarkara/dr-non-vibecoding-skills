# ch04 — The Power of the Corporation

> Explains how the rich use legal structures to protect wealth and minimize taxes.

Taxes are the single largest expense for most people. The rich use the legal structure of a corporation to protect themselves from excessive taxation. An individual earns money, gets taxed, and then tries to live on what is left. A corporation earns money, spends everything it can on legitimate business expenses, and is then taxed on the remainder. This is one of the biggest legal loopholes used by the wealthy. By understanding the law and the tax code, you can use corporations to hold assets and pay for expenses that would otherwise be paid with after-tax dollars. Financial intelligence includes knowledge of accounting, investing, markets, and the law.
