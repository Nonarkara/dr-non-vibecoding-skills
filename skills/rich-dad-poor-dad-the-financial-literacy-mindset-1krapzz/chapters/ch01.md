# ch01 — The Trap of the Rat Race

> Explains how fear and greed drive the cycle of working for a paycheck to pay bills.

Most people are trapped in a cycle called the Rat Race. This cycle is driven by two primary emotions: fear and greed. Fear of not having enough money forces people to work hard at jobs they often dislike. Once they receive a paycheck, greed or desire takes over, leading them to buy things that provide short-term joy but increase their expenses. This creates a need for more money, which leads back to more work. To escape, you must stop reacting to these emotions and start thinking. A job is a short-term solution to a long-term problem. Instead of looking for a bigger paycheck to solve your problems, you must look for ways to build a base of assets that generate income independently of your labor.
