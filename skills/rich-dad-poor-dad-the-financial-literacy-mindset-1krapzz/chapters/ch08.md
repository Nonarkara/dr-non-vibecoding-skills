# ch08 — The Importance of Cash Flow

> Explains why cash flow is more important than net worth or paper gains.

Wealth is not found in the price of your house or the balance of your retirement account if you cannot touch it. True financial freedom comes from cash flow. You must focus on the movement of money. If your assets generate enough monthly cash flow to cover your monthly expenses, you are wealthy. If you have a million dollars in a house but no money for groceries, you are poor. The goal is to build an 'asset engine' that runs without your physical presence. This requires a shift from thinking about 'how much I make' to 'how much my money makes.' Always look at the net cash flow after taxes and expenses are paid.
