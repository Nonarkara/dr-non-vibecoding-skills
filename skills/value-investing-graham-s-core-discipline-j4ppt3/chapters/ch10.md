# ch10 — Summary and Discipline

> Reiterates the core habits: intrinsic value focus, margin of safety, Mr. Market discipline, investing vs speculation, temperament, long‑term view, and continuous learning.

The agent’s edge comes from habit, not brilliance. Each decision should begin with the question ‘What is this business worth?’ followed by a margin‑of‑safety test. Treat daily prices as optional offers from Mr. Market. Confirm that the operation meets Graham’s definition of investment. Guard against emotional impulses by using a pre‑trade checklist. Hold for the long haul, letting compounding work, and review only when fundamentals change. After each trade, document the reasoning and outcome to refine future estimates. By internalizing these habits, the agent can navigate market cycles with consistency and resilience.
