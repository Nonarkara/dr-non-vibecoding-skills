# ch09 — Choosing the Right Method

> Helps the AI decide when a problem is a design problem versus a distribution, pricing, or policy issue.

Not every challenge benefits from HCD. If the core barrier is cost, supply chain, or regulation, the team should first map the problem type: design (needs unknown), distribution (logistics), pricing (economic), or policy (legal). Use a decision tree: ask "Do we need to understand people’s motivations?" If yes, proceed with HCD methods; if no, consider business analysis or advocacy. When a design problem is confirmed, select the most appropriate method based on insight gaps—e.g., use Extreme Users for novel use cases, Conversation Starters for low‑trust environments, or Guided Tours for spatial understanding. This chapter equips the AI with a method‑selection framework.
