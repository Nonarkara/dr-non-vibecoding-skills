# ch10 — From Prototype to Scale

> Describes how to transition a minimum lovable prototype into a scalable solution, including validation and stakeholder alignment.

Scaling begins once a prototype passes usability and desirability tests. Validate the solution with a broader audience using a pilot rollout, measuring adoption, retention, and impact metrics. Align stakeholders—government, NGOs, investors—by sharing evidence from the pilot and co‑creating implementation plans. Identify the minimum viable product (MVP) features needed for scale and separate them from nice‑to‑have enhancements. Finally, create a handoff package that includes research artifacts, design specifications, and a testing protocol for future teams. This chapter gives the AI a checklist to move from a tested prototype to a launch‑ready product.
