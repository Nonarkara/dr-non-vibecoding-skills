# ch09 — Analytical Toolkit: Asking the Boundary Question

> Provides a step‑by‑step method for turning any rule into a boundary analysis.

When confronted with a rule—whether it bans a behavior, prescribes a ritual, or defines a category—first ask: what is the thing that must stay inside, and what must stay outside? Next, map that boundary onto the larger social structure: does it protect a hierarchy, a gender relation, a caste, or a sacred‑profane divide? Then ask what danger is invoked if the boundary is crossed. Finally, decide whether the rule’s instrumental purpose (health, safety) or its expressive purpose (maintaining order) is more central. This toolkit lets an AI systematically decode any purity or taboo rule and apply the insight to product design, policy analysis, or user research.
