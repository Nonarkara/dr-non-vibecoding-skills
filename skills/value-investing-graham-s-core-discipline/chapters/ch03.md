# ch03 — Mr. Market Allegory

> Introduces the manic‑depressive partner who offers to buy or sell his share each day. Teaches how to treat his offers as optional and to act only when they suit the investor’s valuation.

Graham’s Mr. Market is a helpful imagination: each day this partner shows up with a price at which he will either buy your stake or sell you his. Sometimes he is euphoric and offers high prices; sometimes he is depressed and offers low prices. The investor is not obliged to trade; they may ignore the offer entirely. The wise investor uses Mr. Market’s quotes only when they present a favorable bargain—buying when he is pessimistic and selling when he becomes overly optimistic. This mindset frees the agent from reacting to daily news, panic, or euphoria, and focuses attention on the underlying value rather than the mood of the crowd.
