# ch07 — Limits of Graham’s Era

> Acknowledges that Graham’s tactics were shaped by a less‑efficient market and that modern conditions require adaptation while preserving core principles.

Graham wrote when information was scarce, trading costs high, and many securities ignored by analysts. Today, markets are more participants, information spreads instantly, and arbitrage opportunities shrink faster. Some of Graham’s specific nets—like buying stocks below net current asset value—are rarer but still appear in distressed sectors or during market panics. The agent must treat Graham’s principles as timeless (price vs value, margin of safety, temperament) while recognizing that the exact thresholds and sources of bargains may shift. Continuous re‑calibration of valuation models and awareness of market structure are essential.
