# Glossary

Terms and named ideas from the source, anchored to the chapter that explains them.

| Term | Definition | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Intrinsic Value** | The estimated true worth of a business based on its assets, earnings, and cash flows. | ch01 |
| **Market Price** | The current quotation at which a stock trades, reflecting collective sentiment. | ch01 |
| **Margin of Safety** | The percentage by which price falls below a conservative estimate of intrinsic value. | ch02 |
| **Net Current Asset Value (NCAV)** | Current assets minus all liabilities; a liquidation‑based valuation metric. | ch02 |
| **Mr. Market** | An allegorical partner who offers to buy or sell his stake each day at varying prices. | ch03 |
| **Investing** | An operation that, after thorough analysis, promises safety of principal and an adequate return. | ch04 |
| **Speculation** | Any money‑making operation that does not meet the investing definition. | ch04 |
| **Temperament** | The emotional discipline to stick to a plan despite market fear or greed. | ch05 |
| **Compounding** | The process whereby earnings generate additional earnings over time. | ch06 |
| **Bargain Issue** | A stock priced at or below two‑thirds of its net current asset value. | ch09 |
| **Arbitrage** | Simultaneous purchase and sale of equivalent assets to profit from price discrepancies. | ch08 |
| **Diversification** | Spreading capital across many unrelated investments to reduce idiosyncratic risk. | ch08 |
| **FOMO** | Fear of missing out, an emotional urge to buy because others are profiting. | ch05 |
| **Panic** | Sudden fear that leads to selling at depressed prices without analysis. | ch05 |
| **Overconfidence** | Excessive belief in one’s judgment that leads to insufficient margin of safety. | ch05 |
| **Safety of Principal** | A low probability of losing the initial capital invested. | ch04 |
| **Adequate Return** | A return commensurate with the risk taken, not reliant on price momentum alone. | ch04 |
| **Normalized Earnings** | Average earnings over a full business cycle, adjusted for one‑time items. | ch01 |
| **Security Analysis** | Graham and Dodd’s methodology for estimating intrinsic value. | ch01 |
| **Intelligent Investor** | Graham’s guide for applying value‑investing principles to individual investors. | ch01 |
| **Value Investing** | The investment approach of buying securities below their intrinsic value with a margin of safety. | ch01 |
| **Hedge** | A position taken to offset potential losses in another investment. | ch08 |
| **Liquidation Value** | The amount that would be realized if the company’s assets were sold and debts paid. | ch09 |
