# ch05 — Integrating the Routine into Your Workflow

> Provides a step‑by‑step plan for making the unedited drafting method a permanent habit, including tools, timing, and accountability.

To make the unedited drafting method stick, embed it into your daily schedule. Choose a consistent time slot—whether it’s early morning or late night—when you can work without interruption. Use a minimalist writing tool (e.g., plain‑text editor or distraction‑free app) that hides formatting options and forces you to focus on words alone. Begin each session with a brief ritual: a cup of tea, a five‑minute breathing exercise, and a reminder of the core principle—"no editing until tomorrow." After the session, log the word count and note any "[edit]" markers you placed. The next day, allocate a separate block for revision, referencing the log to track progress. If you need external accountability, share your schedule with a writing partner or join a small group that checks in weekly. Over weeks, the routine becomes automatic, and the quality of your first drafts improves dramatically.
