# ch07 — The Skeptic’s Craft

> Questioning traditions and customs to find the 'reasonable reason' behind things.

A writer must be a skeptic. Do not do things just because 'that is how they are done.' Whether it is the custom of meeting in coffee shops or the institution of marriage, ask for the 'reasonable reason.' Immanuel Kant defined enlightenment as the process of emerging from self-imposed immaturity—the habit of letting others think for you. In your writing, challenge the status quo. If a tradition doesn't make sense to you, say so. This critical distance allows you to see the world more clearly and write with more authority.
