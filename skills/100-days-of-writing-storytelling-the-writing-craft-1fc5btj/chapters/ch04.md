# ch04 — Stream of Consciousness

> Using uninterrupted flow to capture the essence of an encounter.

To capture the truth of an experience, you must sometimes write without stopping. This is the stream of consciousness style. The goal is to depict an encounter in a continuous flow, uninterrupted by conventional descriptions or the need to be 'objective.' You write until you physically drop or reach a natural end. This method prevents your logical mind from interrupting the raw data of your experience. You can always come back the next day to edit and organize, but the initial capture must be a pure, flowing movement of thought.
