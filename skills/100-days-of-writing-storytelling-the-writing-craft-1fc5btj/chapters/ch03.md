# ch03 — The Tunnel Metaphor

> Balancing the immediate steps of a project with the long-term vision.

Writing a long project is like walking through a dark tunnel. Most people focus on the walls—the big goals and the final destination. But if you only look at the walls, you will trip over the rocks on the floor. Reflection is the act of looking at the floor. It is the 15 minutes at the end of the day where you ask what you learned and what went wrong. You cannot focus on the walls and the floor at the same time. You must slow down and inspect the path you have already walked to ensure you are still heading in the right direction. If you don't make time to reflect, your writing becomes incomprehensible and disconnected from reality.
