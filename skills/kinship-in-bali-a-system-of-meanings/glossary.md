# Glossary

Terms and named ideas from the source, anchored to the chapter that explains them.

| Term | Definition | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sebel** | A state of ritual pollution or impurity that bars an individual from entering a temple. | ch01 |
| **Pemaksan** | A temple congregation, literally meaning a group bound by 'obligatory work'. | ch01 |
| **Kahyangan Tiga** | The set of three essential temples that form the core of a traditional village unit. | ch02 |
| **Pura Puseh** | The 'Origin Temple' of a village, dedicated to deified ancestors. | ch02 |
| **Pura Dalem** | The 'Death Temple', associated with the cemetery and the souls of the recently deceased. | ch02 |
| **Pura Bale Agung** | The 'Great Council Temple', primarily concerned with agricultural fertility and village meetings. | ch02 |
| **Lontar** | Sacred manuscripts incised on palm leaves, often containing village charters or ritual laws. | ch02 |
| **Banjar** | The hamlet, the primary secular community and administrative unit in Balinese life. | ch03 |
| **Krama Banjar** | The hamlet council, composed of all adult male citizens and their partners. | ch03 |
| **Klian** | An elder or elected chairman of a social group like a hamlet or irrigation society. | ch03 |
| **Subak** | An autonomous irrigation society organized around a shared water source. | ch04 |
| **Triwangsa** | The 'three peoples', referring to the three highest ranks of the gentry. | ch05 |
| **Anak Jero** | Literally 'insiders', a term used to refer to members of the gentry. | ch05 |
| **Anak Jaba** | Literally 'outsiders', a term used to refer to commoners. | ch05 |
| **Pedanda** | A high priest of the Brahmana rank, responsible for creating holy water. | ch07 |
| **Sisia** | A client or follower of a specific high priest. | ch07 |
| **Seka** | A generic term for any organized, functional group or club. | ch08 |
| **Dadia** | A corporate kinship group based on patrilineal descent. | ch09 |
| **Patus** | A specific association within a hamlet dedicated to managing mortuary rituals. | ch03 |
| **Perbekel** | The government-appointed head of a modern administrative village unit. | ch10 |
