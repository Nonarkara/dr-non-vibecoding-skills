# ch07 — Priests and Spiritual Clients

> High priests provide essential ritual services through a non-territorial network of clients who seek holy water and moral guidance.

The Brahmana high priests, or pedanda, occupy the highest rank in the title system and serve as the guardians of sacred knowledge. Unlike temple priests who serve specific congregations, high priests have a personal, often inherited relationship with individual families across a wide region. This relationship is one of 'teacher' and 'student' or 'client.' The priest is the essential source of holy water required for all major ceremonies. In return, clients provide material support and labor for the priest's own family rituals. This network is radically non-territorial; neighbors may serve different priests, and even members of the same household can sometimes have different spiritual allegiances. The priest's role is focused on ritual purity and spiritual tutelage rather than community leadership.
