# ch02 — The Desa Adat and Sacred Law

> The desa adat is a sacred space defined by a set of three temples and a written charter of customary law. It defines the moral boundaries of a locality.

The desa adat is a fundamental unit of social organization centered on the 'Three Great Temples' known as the Kahyangan Tiga. These include the Origin Temple for ancestors, the Death Temple for the souls of the deceased, and the Great Council Temple for agricultural fertility. The desa adat functions primarily as a legal and symbolic entity. It preserves a written charter, often inscribed on palm leaves, which dictates rules for behavior, marriage, inheritance, and ritual purity. These regulations vary significantly from one locality to another, creating a strong sense of local identity. While it defines the sacred boundaries and customary laws of an area, it often delegates the actual implementation of these rules to smaller, more active community units like the hamlet.
