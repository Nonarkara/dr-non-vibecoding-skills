# ch06 — The Director

> The role that sets the stage, gathers the cast, and keeps the project moving toward a goal.

The Director is the person who sees the big picture. While the Collaborator focuses on the team's internal dynamics, the Director focuses on the project's overall trajectory. They are responsible for assembling the right mix of personas for a specific challenge. A Director knows when to push the team for more ideas and when to start narrowing down the options. they are the ones who secure the resources and the 'political' cover needed for the team to do its work. They don't just manage; they orchestrate. They ensure that the 'Learning' roles have enough time to gather insights and that the 'Building' roles have the materials they need to create. The Director's success is measured by the team's ability to deliver a finished, impactful result.
