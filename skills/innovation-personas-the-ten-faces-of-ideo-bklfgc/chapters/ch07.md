# ch07 — The Experience Architect

> The role that maps out every touchpoint of a customer's interaction with a brand.

The Experience Architect is a building role that looks beyond the product to the entire service ecosystem. They design the 'customer journey' (the specific sequence of events a person experiences). They are obsessed with the details that others might overlook, such as the smell of a lobby, the tone of a confirmation email, or the ease of opening a package. They use 'emotional stickers' or maps to track how a user feels at each stage of an interaction—where they are frustrated, where they are delighted, and where they are bored. The Experience Architect's goal is to create a seamless, memorable experience that turns a customer into a fan. They understand that in a world of commodities, the experience is the only true differentiator.
