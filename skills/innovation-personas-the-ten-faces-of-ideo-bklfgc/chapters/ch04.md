# ch04 — The Hurdler

> The role that finds creative ways to bypass organizational roadblocks and resource constraints.

The Hurdler is an organizing role that thrives on constraints. While others see a lack of budget or a strict regulation as a reason to stop, the Hurdler sees it as a challenge to find a workaround. They are the masters of the 'quiet' innovation—making progress without waiting for official permission or a massive budget. They know how to turn a 'no' into a 'how might we.' A Hurdler doesn't just work harder; they work smarter by finding the path of least resistance. They are essential in large organizations where bureaucracy often kills ideas before they can be tested. They protect the spark of an idea from the 'Devil's Advocate' by finding small, undeniable wins that prove the concept's value before the critics can shut it down.
