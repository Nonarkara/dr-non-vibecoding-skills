# Glossary

Terms and named ideas from the source, anchored to the chapter that explains them.

| Term | Definition | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Universal Grammar** | A set of innate grammatical principles that all human languages share. | ch01 |
| **Pidgin** | A simplified language that emerges when speakers of different tongues need to communicate. | ch02 |
| **Creole** | A fully developed language that originates from a pidgin when children adopt it as their native tongue. | ch02 |
| **Sign Language** | A visual‑manual language with its own grammar, not a gestural translation of spoken language. | ch03 |
| **Anchoring** | The tendency to rely heavily on the first piece of information encountered. | ch04 |
| **Availability Heuristic** | Judging the likelihood of events by how easily examples come to mind. | ch04 |
| **Framing Effect** | Changing decisions by presenting the same information as a gain or a loss. | ch04 |
| **Motivated Reasoning** | Filtering evidence to protect pre‑existing beliefs or identities. | ch04 |
| **WYSIATI** | The bias to treat the information currently visible as the whole story. | ch04 |
| **Pre‑mortem** | A technique that imagines a future failure and lists ways it could happen. | ch05 |
| **Base‑Rate Framing** | Presenting the prior probability before new evidence to aid proper Bayesian updating. | ch05 |
| **Reference Class Forecasting** | Comparing a situation to a statistically similar set of past cases to estimate outcomes. | ch05 |
| **Four‑fold Pattern** | A decision matrix that combines probability (high/low) with impact (high/low). | ch05 |
| **Phrase Structure** | The hierarchical organization of words into noun phrases, verb phrases, and clauses. | ch06 |
| **Agreement Suffix (-s)** | A morphological marker in English that signals third‑person singular present tense. | ch06 |
| **Dialect** | A systematic variation of a language tied to a social or geographic group. | ch07 |
| **African American Vernacular English (AAVE)** | A dialect with its own grammatical rules, such as negative concord. | ch07 |
| **Working Memory** | The limited capacity system that holds chunks of information while processing language. | ch08 |
| **Predictive Coding** | The brain’s tendency to anticipate upcoming words based on context. | ch08 |
| **Confidence Interval** | A range that expresses the uncertainty around a point estimate. | ch09 |
| **Point Estimate** | A single numeric claim without an accompanying measure of uncertainty. | ch09 |
| **Motivated Bias** | A systematic distortion that protects self‑image or group identity. | ch09 |
| **Subject‑Verb Agreement** | The rule that the verb form must match the subject’s number and person. | ch06 |
| **Topic Marking** | A grammatical device that signals which part of a sentence is the focus. | ch03 |
| **Auxiliary Movement** | The process of moving an auxiliary verb to form a question or other constructions. | ch06 |
