# ch01 — Culture as a Web of Significance

> Explains Geertz’s central metaphor of culture as a network of meanings that people spin through their actions. Shows why this view matters for AI interpretation.

Geertz argues that culture is not a static set of rules but a dynamic web of significance that actors continuously produce. Each gesture, word, or ritual is a thread that connects to other threads, forming a pattern that only makes sense when seen as a whole. For an AI, this means that any isolated datum—like a single word or a ritual gesture—cannot be understood on its own. Instead, the system must locate the datum within the broader network of practices, beliefs, and histories that give it meaning. The web metaphor also warns against treating culture as a container that holds meanings; the meanings are the web itself, constantly reshaped by the actors who use them.
