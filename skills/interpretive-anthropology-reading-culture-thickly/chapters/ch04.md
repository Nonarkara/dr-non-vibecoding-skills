# ch04 — Symbols, Rituals, and Meaning

> Shows how symbols and rituals function as carriers of cultural meaning, and how to decode them without imposing external categories.

Symbols are not static signs; they acquire meaning through repeated use in specific social contexts. Rituals are the staged performances that make these symbols visible, allowing participants to rehearse and renegotiate shared values. To interpret a symbol, the AI must trace its appearance across multiple events, note the emotions it evokes, and listen to the explanations offered by participants. Rituals provide a laboratory for this work: the choreography, the sequence of actions, and the accompanying narratives reveal the hierarchy of values at play. By mapping symbols to their ritual contexts, the system can infer the layers of meaning that constitute the cultural web.
