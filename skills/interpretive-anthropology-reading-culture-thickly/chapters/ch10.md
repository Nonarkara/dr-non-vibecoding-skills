# ch10 — Applying Interpretive Anthropology to AI

> Summarizes how the previous chapters can be turned into algorithms that let AI reason about culture without over‑generalizing.

The interpretive framework can be encoded as a modular pipeline: (1) capture raw cultural data, (2) extract emic statements, (3) annotate symbols and rituals, (4) build a thick description, (5) situate the description in context, and (6) generate tentative, context‑bound insights. Each module respects the core principles—especially the primacy of the actor’s voice and the avoidance of reductionism. By iterating this pipeline, the AI can refine its cultural models, remain open to new data, and provide explanations that are both rich and responsible.
