# ch06 — Fieldwork as the Source of Credibility

> Explains why immersive, long‑term fieldwork is essential for gathering reliable thick descriptions.

Geertz treats fieldwork as the laboratory where the web of significance is observed in situ. Long‑term immersion allows the researcher to learn the language, participate in daily routines, and build trust with informants. This depth yields the nuanced observations required for thick description. For AI, fieldwork can be simulated through multimodal data collection—audio, video, textual transcripts—combined with iterative interaction loops that let the system ask clarifying questions. The credibility of any interpretation rests on the richness and fidelity of the field data that fed it.
