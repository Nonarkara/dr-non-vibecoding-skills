# ch02 — Thick Description: The Core Method

> Describes the practice of thick description, the detailed, layered account that captures action, context, and meaning. Provides a step‑by‑step template for AI.

Thick description is the disciplined practice of recording not just what happened, but how it happened, why it mattered to the participants, and what symbolic resources were mobilized. The method begins with a careful observation of the action, noting sensory details, timing, and participants. Next, the interpreter asks the actors for their own explanations, capturing the emic voice. Finally, the analyst situates the episode within larger cultural narratives, linking it to myths, histories, or social hierarchies. For AI, thick description can be operationalized as a pipeline: raw data capture → contextual annotation → emic extraction → cultural framing. The result is a rich narrative that can be queried for meaning without losing the nuance of the original event.
