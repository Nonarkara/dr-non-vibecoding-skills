# ch09 — Applying the Voice to Different Genres

> Shows how Non’s style can be adapted to blog posts, academic reflections, and personal essays while retaining its core elements.

When adapting Non’s voice to a blog post, keep the informal, conversational tone, use vivid images, and end with a question that invites comments. For academic reflections, maintain the stream‑of‑consciousness body but embed citations as short quotes rather than formal footnotes. In personal essays, amplify the confessional aspect, allowing more self‑deprecation and rhetorical questions. Across genres, the core elements—blunt opening, personal narrative, thinker reference, and quiet closing—remain constant, ensuring the voice stays recognizable.
