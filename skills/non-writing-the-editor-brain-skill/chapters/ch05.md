# ch05 — Signature Transitions and Rhythm

> Details the recurring transition phrases and rhythmic patterns that give Non’s prose its unique flow.

Non’s transitions are rarely conventional connectors like "however" or "therefore." Instead, he uses personal asides, dashes, or short clauses that feel like spoken interjections: "That’s why I think…"; "In other words, …"; "So, I thought to myself…" These phrases act as bridges while preserving the conversational tone. Rhythmically, sentences alternate between short, punchy statements and longer, winding clauses that contain multiple ideas. The writer often inserts a parenthetical comment or a brief quote to break the flow and add texture. This pattern creates a cadence that feels like a spoken monologue, with natural pauses and emphases.
