# ch06 — Do/Don’t Rules: What Non Does Right, What to Avoid

> Lists concrete do and don’t guidelines for reproducing Non’s style, focusing on authenticity, self‑reference, and avoiding generic polish.

Do write as if you are speaking to yourself; include personal anecdotes, self‑questioning, and occasional self‑deprecation. Do embed short quotes or thinker references that act as intellectual side‑notes. Do use vivid images to anchor abstract ideas. Don’t employ generic transitions or overly formal academic language. Don’t smooth out the rough edges; keep the occasional grammatical stumble or run‑on that mirrors real thought. Don’t hide the writer’s voice behind a neutral tone; the essay must always feel like Non is present, commenting on his own process.
