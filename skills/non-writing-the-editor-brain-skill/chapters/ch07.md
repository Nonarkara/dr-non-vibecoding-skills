# ch07 — Signature Techniques: Quotes, Dialogue, Contrast, and Before/After

> Explores the recurring tools Non uses—short quotes, embedded dialogue, contrast/reversal, and the before/after move—to enrich his prose.

Non frequently drops a short quote, often from a philosopher or a pop culture source, to punctuate a point. He also inserts brief dialogue snippets, usually a single line of imagined speech, to dramatize an internal debate. Contrast and reversal appear when he juxtaposes two opposing ideas in the same paragraph, e.g., "the bike frees me, yet it also confines me to the road." The before/after move frames a personal transformation: he describes a situation before a bike ride, then after, highlighting the shift in perception. These techniques create texture and keep the reader engaged with varied micro‑structures.
