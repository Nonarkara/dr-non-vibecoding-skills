# Glossary

Terms and named ideas from the source, anchored to the chapter that explains them.

| Term | Definition | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **blunt_open** | A paragraph that starts with a stark, unapologetic claim. | ch02 |
| **image_hook** | Opening with a vivid visual description that draws the reader in. | ch02 |
| **thinker_load** | Dropping a philosopher or author’s name early to carry intellectual weight. | ch02 |
| **stream_consciousness** | Writing a continuous, unedited flow of thoughts before inserting analysis. | ch03 |
| **before_after** | Framing a transformation by describing a situation before and after an event. | ch07 |
| **contrast_reversal** | Juxtaposing two opposing ideas in the same paragraph to highlight tension. | ch07 |
| **embedded_dialogue** | A short imagined line of speech inserted to dramatize internal debate. | ch07 |
| **quiet_aphorism** | A short, reflective statement used to close an essay. | ch04 |
| **open_ended_question** | A rhetorical question at the end that leaves the thought unresolved. | ch04 |
| **Barthes** | Semioticist whose idea of "Writing Degree Zero" influences Non’s view of writing as an end. | ch01 |
| **Murakami** | Japanese novelist whose running memoir inspires Non’s biking metaphors. | ch05 |
| **Hume** | Philosopher whose internal empiricism is cited when discussing perception. | ch08 |
| **Kant** | Philosopher whose ideas on morality and marriage shape Non’s love essays. | ch08 |
| **Non** | The author’s pen name, treated as a persona that talks directly to the reader. | ch01 |
