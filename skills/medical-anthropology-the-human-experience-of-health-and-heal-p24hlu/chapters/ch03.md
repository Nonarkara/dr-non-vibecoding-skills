# ch03 — Idioms of Distress

> How culture shapes the expression of stress and psychological pain.

Stress is not a universal biological constant but is enacted and spoken about in varied ways. People use specific 'idioms of distress' to describe their suffering. For example, 'nervios' in Latin America or 'pusing' in Indonesia are culturally specific ways to communicate being overwhelmed by social or economic hardship. These idioms allow people to express pain that might be stigmatized if labeled as a mental health disorder. In some contexts, a diagnosis like depression can validate socioeconomic suffering, while in others, it may be rejected. Understanding these idioms is crucial for clinicians to provide effective support that resonates with the patient's cultural framework.
