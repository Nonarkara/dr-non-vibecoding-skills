# ch07 — The Movement of Medicines

> Tracing how drugs flow through informal markets and local economies.

Medicines do not only move through official pharmacies and doctors' prescriptions. Synthetic drugs and natural remedies flow through pharmaceutical promotion, small business ventures, and illegal trade. People consume medicines according to their own reasoning, often using them 'off-label' or mixing them with herbal supplements. In many parts of the world, the corner market stall is a more accessible source of healthcare than a hospital. Medical anthropologists study how people circumvent regulations to access care and how the global pharmaceutical industry interacts with these local, informal marketplaces.
