# ch07 — Technology Supply vs. Market Demand

> Describes how performance overshoot changes the basis of competition and creates openings for disruptors.

When a product’s performance improves faster than customers’ willingness to pay for that performance, the market’s basis of competition shifts—from functionality to reliability, then convenience, and finally price. This overshoot creates a vacuum at lower price points that disruptive entrants can fill. Companies that monitor the gap between technology supply and market demand can anticipate when the basis of competition will change and pre‑emptively develop lower‑margin offerings.
