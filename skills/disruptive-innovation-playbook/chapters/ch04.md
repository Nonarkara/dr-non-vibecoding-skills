# ch04 — Small Markets, Big Growth Constraints

> Explains why large firms cannot rely on small emerging markets for growth and must delegate them to appropriately sized teams.

A $4 billion company needs hundreds of millions in new revenue to grow 20 %, while a $40 million firm needs only a few million. Small, fast‑growing markets cannot satisfy the growth targets of large incumbents, leading them to wait until the market is “big enough.” This waiting period often allows entrants to dominate. The remedy is to match the size of the organization tackling the market to the market’s size—small, focused teams can move quickly, allocate talent, and capture first‑mover advantage without being hampered by the bureaucracy of a large corporation.
