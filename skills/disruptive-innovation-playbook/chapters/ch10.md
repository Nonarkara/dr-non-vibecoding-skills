# ch10 — Actionable Playbook

> Provides a step‑by‑step guide to apply the Innovator’s Dilemma concepts in any organization.

The final chapter translates theory into practice: (1) Scan for technologies that outperform current performance trajectories; (2) Identify emerging customer segments that value different attributes; (3) Form an autonomous unit with a cost structure matching the new market; (4) Use discovery‑based planning to test business models; (5) Monitor the performance‑demand gap to anticipate shifts in competition; (6) Reallocate resources once learning validates the opportunity. The playbook equips managers with concrete actions to avoid the classic trap of over‑investing in sustaining innovation while missing disruptive threats.
