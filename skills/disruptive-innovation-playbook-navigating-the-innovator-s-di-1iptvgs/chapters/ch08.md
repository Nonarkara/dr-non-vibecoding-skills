# ch08 — Spotting Disruptive Threats – The EV Case

> Applies the framework to a real‑world scenario: electric vehicles in the automotive industry.

The electric vehicle (EV) illustrates how a seemingly niche, low‑margin technology can become disruptive. By establishing an autonomous EV program, aligning cost structures with low‑margin expectations, and targeting emerging markets (e.g., regulatory mandates, urban commuters), an incumbent can avoid being blindsided. The case study demonstrates the steps: create a separate unit, adopt discovery‑based planning, and monitor performance‑demand gaps.
