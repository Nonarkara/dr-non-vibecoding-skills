# ch06 — Capabilities vs. Disabilities

> Shows how an organization’s processes and values can become obstacles when tackling disruptive problems.

People can be retrained, but processes and values are sticky. A firm that excels at high‑margin product development cannot easily shift to low‑margin, high‑volume production because its existing processes (e.g., design reviews, cost accounting) and values (e.g., prioritizing performance over cost) are misaligned with the new problem. The chapter provides a framework to map where capabilities reside and how to create new capabilities—either by building new processes, altering values, or establishing separate units—to overcome these disabilities.
