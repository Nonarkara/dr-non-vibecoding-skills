# ch03 — The Resource Allocation Trap

> Shows why mainstream organizations cannot fund disruptive projects and how autonomous units solve the problem.

Incumbent firms allocate capital based on the needs of their existing customers, which leads to a bias toward high‑margin, high‑performance projects. When a disruptive technology requires a low‑margin, low‑cost business model, the existing organization’s processes and incentives block investment. The solution is to create an autonomous unit—separate budget, staff, and cost structure—focused on the new market. This unit is free from the power of the incumbent’s customers and can align its resources with the resource‑dependence dynamics of the emerging market, allowing it to compete effectively at low margins.
