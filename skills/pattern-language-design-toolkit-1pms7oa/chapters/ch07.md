# ch07 — Creating Your Own Pattern Language

> Encourages users to develop personal or community‑specific pattern languages based on the core catalogue.

The published language is a starting point, not a final authority. Each society, community, or individual will eventually craft their own pattern language that reflects their unique values, experiences, and environment. To begin, one can take the core patterns that feel universal—such as ACCESSIBLE GREEN or PRIVATE TERRACE—and adapt or extend them with new patterns that address local needs. Over time, these new patterns become part of the living language, and the original patterns may be refined or replaced. This process keeps the language alive, relevant, and deeply rooted in the people who use it.
