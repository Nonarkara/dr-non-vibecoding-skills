# Cheatsheet

Imperative decision rules from the source, grouped by category. Each is the kind of sentence you would write on an index card.

## process

- If you have not been laughed at, you have not been listening.
- Record observations immediately; do not trust your memory to stay messy.
- Study the full seasonal cycle before claiming to understand the system.
- If the task feels too simple, you are missing the ritual.

## general

- Observe the imponderabilia of actual life, not just the formal rules.
- Be a 'fair and true interpreter' of the user's reality.

## design

- Distinguish between what people say they do and what they actually do.
- Physical elevation equals social rank; watch the heads.
- Aesthetics often trump utility in the eyes of the user.
- Cooking and wealth-storage should be physically separated.

## product

- Wealth is for movement, not for accumulation.
- Every industry has a traditional owner; respect the monopoly.
- Surplus is a social tool, not a waste.

## writing

- The 'obvious' is the most valuable data point.
- Use 'solicitary gifts' to trigger the information you need.
- The 'colouring' of a fact is as important as the fact itself.

## argument

- Follow the matrilineal line to find the real power.

## life

- The 'tent' must be in the village, not on the veranda.
- A gift always demands a return, but the timing is the art.
- The researcher's alienation is a data point, not a failure.
