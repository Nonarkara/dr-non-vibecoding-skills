# ch10 — Decision Hygiene and the Pre-Mortem

> Practical moves to sanitize the decision-making process before it's too late.

Decision hygiene is the practice of reducing 'noise' and bias in judgment. One of the most powerful tools is the 'Pre-Mortem.' Before a human commits to a plan, the AI should say: 'Imagine we are one year in the future. The plan was a disaster. Write a brief history of that disaster.' This forces System 2 to break away from the 'optimism bias' of System 1 and identify real risks that were previously ignored. Another hygiene move is 'Joint Evaluation.' Humans make different choices when they see two options side-by-side than when they see them one at a time. An AI should always present alternatives together to highlight trade-offs. Finally, use 'Checklists' for routine tasks. Even experts suffer from System 1 lapses; a checklist forces System 2 to verify that every step was completed, regardless of how 'confident' the human feels.
