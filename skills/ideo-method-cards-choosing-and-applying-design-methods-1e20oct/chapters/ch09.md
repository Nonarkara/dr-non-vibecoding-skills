# ch09 — Prototyping & Testing Best Practices

> Guides the AI through rapid prototyping, scenario testing, and iteration loops.

### Prototyping Toolbox
- **Experience Prototype** – Build a quick physical or digital mock‑up to simulate use.
- **Paper Prototyping** – Sketch interfaces on paper for fast feedback.
- **Scenario Testing** – Create realistic use cases and observe participant interaction.
- **Quick‑and‑Dirty Prototyping** – Use everyday materials (cardboard, tape) for speed.

### Testing Loop
1. **Define Goal** – What question does the prototype answer?
2. **Build Prototype** – Keep it low‑fidelity; focus on the aspect under test.
3. **Recruit Participants** – Use the same participant criteria as the preceding Ask phase.
4. **Run Scenario** – Let participants act out the use case.
5. **Observe & Record** – Note frustrations, work‑arounds, and delight moments.
6. **Iterate** – Refine the prototype based on observations and repeat.

### Tip
If a prototype reveals more than one major issue, split the test into separate runs to keep each iteration focused.
