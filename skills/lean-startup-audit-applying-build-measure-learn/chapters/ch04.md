# ch04 — Learning and Decision Making

> Shows how to interpret data, decide whether to persevere or pivot, and embed learning into the product roadmap.

Learning occurs when the measured data confirms or refutes the original hypothesis. The chapter outlines a decision framework: if the activation metric meets or exceeds the target, the hypothesis is validated and the team can *persevere*—continue building out the product. If the metric falls short, the team must *pivot*—change one or more elements of the hypothesis while keeping the core learning loop intact. Common pivot types include zoom‑in (target a narrower segment), zoom‑out (broaden the market), or technology pivot (change the underlying tech). The chapter provides a checklist for pivot decisions: review metric trends, assess alternative hypotheses, estimate the cost of change, and align with the overall vision. It also recommends documenting each learning outcome in a shared knowledge base to avoid repeating mistakes.
