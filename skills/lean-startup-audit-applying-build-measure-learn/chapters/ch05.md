# ch05 — Innovation Accounting and Scaling

> Introduces the accounting system that turns raw metrics into business‑level insight, enabling startups to scale responsibly.

Innovation accounting is a three‑step process that translates validated learning into financial and strategic decisions. First, establish a *baseline* by measuring the current state of the key metric. Second, set a *learning milestone*—a target improvement that indicates the hypothesis is moving in the right direction. Third, evaluate *growth* by comparing the new metric against the baseline after a defined period. The chapter shows how to build a simple dashboard that tracks activation, retention, and revenue cohorts side by side, allowing founders to see whether the business is truly moving toward a sustainable model. It also explains how to use this data to justify further investment, allocate resources, and communicate progress to stakeholders. By treating learning as a measurable asset, startups can scale with confidence rather than guesswork.
