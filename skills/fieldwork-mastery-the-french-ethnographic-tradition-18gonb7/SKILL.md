## Core principles

- The ethnographic fact outlasts the academic theory.
- True observation requires treating the familiar as if it were entirely alien.
- Physical presence and shared experience are the only ways to access certain types of knowledge.
- Social structures are best understood through the specific, singular details of daily life.
- The researcher is a biological instrument whose own reactions are part of the data.
- Ritual is a sequence of movements through space and social states, not just a set of beliefs.
- Small-scale communities allow for a total understanding of social networks that large-scale studies miss.
- The autonomy of the symbolic means culture follows its own logic, independent of geography or economics.
