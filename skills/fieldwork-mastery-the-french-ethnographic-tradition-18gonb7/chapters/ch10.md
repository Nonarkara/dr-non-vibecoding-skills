# ch10 — The Narrative and the Unconscious

> Extracting cultural memory through informal storytelling.

Standardized questionnaires often fail to capture the true depth of a culture. Instead, a researcher should allow informants to take 'short cuts' and follow their own memories. This informal approach acts like a mnemotechnical prompt, allowing the informant to reveal 'unconscious' cultural memories that they might not share in a formal interview. You should pay attention to the rhythm of their speech, their syntax, and the way they tell stories. Sometimes, a novelist or a local storyteller provides a better ethnographic record than a scientist because they capture the 'texture' of life. By listening to how people talk about their past and their daily lives, you can access a level of detail that is missing from official records. This requires patience and a willingness to let the informant lead the conversation.
