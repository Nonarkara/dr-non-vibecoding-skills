# ch02 — Four Laws of Behavior Change

> Breaks down each law—obvious, attractive, easy, satisfying—and gives concrete tactics for applying them to new or existing habits.

## Four Laws of Behavior Change

1. **Make it Obvious** – Place visual cues where the behavior will happen. Example: leave workout clothes by the bed to remind you to exercise each morning. For an AI, generate reminders tied to existing calendar events.
2. **Make it Attractive** – Pair the habit with something you enjoy (temptation bundling). If you love coffee, only sip it while reviewing a flashcard deck.
3. **Make it Easy** – Reduce friction. Break a habit into the smallest possible step (e.g., write one sentence instead of a full report). Automate prerequisites with scripts or templates.
4. **Make it Satisfying** – Provide immediate reward. Tick a checklist, earn a point, or enjoy a short break after completion. The brain learns faster when the outcome feels good.

Applying the laws in sequence creates a habit loop that is both triggerable and reinforcing. The assistant can audit a user’s routine, flag missing cues, suggest attractive pairings, streamline steps, and deliver instant feedback, ensuring each law is satisfied before moving to the next.
