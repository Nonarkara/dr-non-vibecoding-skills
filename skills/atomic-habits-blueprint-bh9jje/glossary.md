# Glossary

Terms and named ideas from the source, anchored to the chapter that explains them.

| Term | Definition | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Compound Growth** | The exponential increase that results from applying a small percentage improvement daily. | ch01 |
| **Cue** | A trigger that signals the brain to start a habit. | ch04 |
| **Craving** | The desire that motivates the habit response. | ch04 |
| **Response** | The actual behavior performed after a cue and craving. | ch04 |
| **Reward** | The outcome that satisfies the craving and reinforces the habit. | ch04 |
| **Four Laws** | Make it obvious, attractive, easy, and satisfying. | ch02 |
| **Identity‑Based Habit** | A habit tied to the person you want to become rather than a distant outcome. | ch03 |
| **Habit Stacking** | Pairing a new habit with an existing routine to leverage an established cue. | ch05 |
| **Environment Design** | Arranging physical or digital surroundings to highlight good cues and hide bad ones. | ch05 |
| **Temptation Bundling** | Combining a habit you want to build with an activity you enjoy. | ch02 |
| **Micro‑Behavior** | The smallest possible version of a habit that can be done in seconds. | ch03 |
| **Friction** | Any obstacle that makes a habit harder to perform. | ch05 |
| **Immediate Reward** | A quick, tangible benefit that follows a habit, reinforcing the loop. | ch02 |
| **Self‑Concept** | The mental image of who you are, which drives identity‑based habits. | ch03 |
| **Cue Visibility** | How prominently a trigger appears in the environment. | ch05 |
