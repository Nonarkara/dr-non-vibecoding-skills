# ch09 — Phonesthetics and Uncommon Words

> Explores how word sound influences meaning and how to choose striking yet understandable vocabulary.

Phonesthetics is the feeling that a word’s sound matches its meaning—*haunting* sounds eerie, *tart* sounds sharp. Selecting words with appropriate sound can reinforce the image you are painting. Use uncommon words sparingly and only when they add precision or a memorable auditory cue. A dictionary or thesaurus can help, but always test the word in a sentence to ensure the reader will grasp it without a lookup.
