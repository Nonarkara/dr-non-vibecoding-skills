# ch10 — Putting It All Together: A Checklist for Classic Prose

> Provides a practical, step‑by‑step checklist that writers can apply before publishing any piece of prose.

Before you finish, run through this checklist: 1) Identify the concrete scene you want to show. 2) Replace any nominalizations with active verbs. 3) Choose the voice (active or passive) that best highlights the intended focus. 4) Scan for hedges and remove unnecessary ones. 5) Trim metadiscourse and signposts to the essential. 6) Swap clichés for fresh images or literal descriptions. 7) Verify that each sentence uses a noun for things and a verb for actions. 8) Test any uncommon words for clarity and sound. 9) Read the passage aloud to catch awkward rhythms. 10) Ensure the overall flow feels like a conversation with a knowledgeable peer.
