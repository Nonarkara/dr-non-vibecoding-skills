# ch03 — Aligning Writing with Long‑Term Projects

> Links the daily habit to the overarching goal of completing a dissertation or any multi‑year undertaking.

The author frames the PhD dissertation as a “life‑long project” that benefits from the same incremental progress as daily blog posts. By treating each entry as a research note, a literature snippet, or a draft paragraph, the larger manuscript gradually assembles. The chapter advises breaking the dissertation into three layers: (1) core ideas (theoretical framework), (2) supporting evidence (case studies, field notes), and (3) polished chapters. Daily writing should target whichever layer needs the most attention that day. A weekly review aligns the micro‑outputs with the macro‑timeline, catching drift early and keeping the project on schedule.
