# ch07 — Balancing Mental Health and Work

> Offers practical routines for maintaining well‑being while pursuing demanding academic or professional goals.

The author describes periods of burnout during the PhD, attributing them to isolation, financial strain, and relentless work. To counteract this, a three‑part routine is recommended: (1) a daily “mind‑reset” of 5 minutes of breathing or meditation, (2) a weekly “social recharge” where you meet a friend or attend a community event, and (3) a monthly “reflection retreat” in a different environment (e.g., a park or a quiet café). These rituals create psychological distance from the work, preventing the sense of being trapped in a single identity and preserving the creative spark needed for research.
