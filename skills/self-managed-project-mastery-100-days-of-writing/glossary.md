# Glossary

Terms and named ideas from the source, anchored to the chapter that explains them.

| Term | Definition | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Aphorism** | A short, memorable statement that captures an insight. | ch06 |
| **Synthetic a priori** | Knowledge that is true before experience and guides action. | ch05 |
| **Knowledge worker** | Someone whose primary output is ideas, analysis, or information. | ch01 |
| **Timeboxing** | Allocating a fixed amount of time to a task and stopping when the box expires. | ch08 |
| **Milestone** | A concrete, measurable checkpoint in a larger project. | ch03 |
| **Urban distraction** | Noise, competition, and superficial interactions that drain focus. | ch02 |
| **Focus sanctuary** | A quiet place used deliberately to boost concentration. | ch02 |
| **Decision filter** | A short list of criteria used to accept or reject a task. | ch04 |
| **Project habit** | A regular, low‑effort activity that keeps a larger goal moving forward. | ch01 |
| **Mental reset** | A brief pause for breathing or meditation to clear the mind. | ch07 |
| **Reflection retreat** | A longer period away from routine to review goals and values. | ch07 |
| **PhD** | A research doctorate, often pursued for deep expertise or academic credentials. | ch04 |
