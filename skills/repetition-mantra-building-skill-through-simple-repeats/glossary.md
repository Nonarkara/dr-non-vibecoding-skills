# Glossary

Terms and named ideas from the source, anchored to the chapter that explains them.

| Term | Definition | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **mantra** | A short, repeatable phrase used as a mental anchor for practice. | ch01 |
| **repetition routine** | A scheduled plan that specifies how many times to repeat the mantra and when to do it. | ch02 |
| **trigger** | An existing habit or cue that prompts you to start the mantra practice. | ch02 |
| **fluency** | The state where repeating the mantra feels effortless and can be done while thinking about other things. | ch03 |
| **micro‑goal** | A small, immediate challenge within a session, such as ten flawless repetitions in a row. | ch04 |
| **sensory channel** | The mode through which you engage the mantra—speaking, whispering, writing, or gesturing. | ch04 |
| **metronome use** | Employing the mantra’s steady rhythm to time another activity, like a musical scale or coding sprint. | ch05 |
| **chunking** | Breaking a larger skill into tiny, repeatable pieces that mirror the mantra’s structure. | ch05 |
| **log** | A simple record of date, target repetitions, actual count, and brief mood note for each session. | ch02 |
| **mantra vacation** | A planned break or reduced‑target day to reset motivation when monotony builds. | ch04 |
| **warm‑up block** | A short period of mantra repetition at the start of a practice session to prepare focus. | ch05 |
| **cool‑down block** | A brief mantra repetition at the end of a session to signal completion and aid transition. | ch05 |
