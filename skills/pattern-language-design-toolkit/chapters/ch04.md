# ch04 — Asterisks and Invariants

> Clarifies the meaning of the asterisk markings and how to treat each level of confidence.

Each pattern is marked with either two asterisks (**), one asterisk (*), or none. ** indicates that the authors believe the solution captures an invariant—a property that must be present for any successful solution. Ignoring such a pattern will likely lead to failure. * signals a provisional solution; designers should treat it as a strong suggestion but be open to improvement. No asterisk means the solution is an example, not a claim of necessity; designers are encouraged to explore alternative solutions. Understanding these markings helps prioritize which patterns to follow strictly and which to experiment with, fostering both reliability and creativity.
