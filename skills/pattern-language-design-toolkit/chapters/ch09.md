# ch09 — Building‑Level and Room‑Level Patterns

> Covers the patterns that guide the design of building complexes, individual houses, and interior spaces.

From the overall building complex to the placement of windows, roofs, and interior gradients, this chapter walks through the sequence of patterns that shape the built form. Key patterns include Building Complex, Site Repair, Main Entrance, Courtyards Which Live, and Room‑Specific patterns such as Couple’s Realm or Private Terrace. Each pattern defines relationships—between sun, wind, circulation, and human activity—that must be respected. By applying them in order, designers create buildings that feel coherent, functional, and connected to the surrounding town patterns.
