# ch07 — Overcoming the Five Obstacles

> Identifies the psychological barriers to financial success.

Even with financial literacy, people face five main obstacles: fear, cynicism, laziness, bad habits, and arrogance. Fear of losing money is common, but the difference between a rich person and a poor person is how they handle that fear. The rich use failure as a teacher; the poor let it crush them. Cynicism (the 'chicken little' syndrome) keeps people from seeing opportunities because they focus on what could go wrong. Laziness often shows up as staying busy with unimportant things to avoid the hard work of managing one's finances. To succeed, you must develop the habit of paying yourself first and remain humble enough to keep learning rather than assuming you know everything.
