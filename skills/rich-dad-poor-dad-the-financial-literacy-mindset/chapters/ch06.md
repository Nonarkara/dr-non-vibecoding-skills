# ch06 — Work to Learn, Not to Earn

> Advocates for skill acquisition over job security and high pay.

When choosing a job, look for what you will learn rather than what you will earn. Many people are 'one skill away from wealth' but fail because they specialize too deeply in a single technical area. To be successful, you need a broad range of skills: sales, marketing, communication, and an understanding of financial systems. The goal is to learn how to manage people, systems, and money. If you are a great writer but cannot sell, you will be a 'poor help-wanted writer.' If you learn to sell, you become a 'best-selling author.' Do not work for the sake of security; work to acquire the skills necessary to run your own business and manage your own assets.
