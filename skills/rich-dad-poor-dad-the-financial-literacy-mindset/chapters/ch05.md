# ch05 — Inventing Money

> Focuses on seeing opportunities and creating value where others see nothing.

The rich 'invent' money by identifying opportunities that others miss. This requires financial creativity and the courage to act. Most people only see the obvious path: work hard, save money, and borrow. The financially intelligent look for ways to put deals together, find money for investments without using a bank, and organize smart people to work for them. In the real world, it is often not the smart who get ahead, but the bold. If you are waiting for the 'right' opportunity to fall into your lap, you may wait forever. You must train your brain to see things that others do not see. The more you exercise your financial mind, the more wealth you can create out of thin air.
