# ch03 — Minding Your Own Business

> Distinguishes between a profession and a business.

There is a significant difference between your profession and your business. Your profession is what you do to earn a paycheck (e.g., being a lawyer, a teacher, or a mechanic). Your business is your asset column. Most people spend their lives working in someone else's business and making that person rich. To become financially secure, you must mind your own business by keeping your expenses low, reducing your liabilities, and diligently building a base of solid assets. These assets can include stocks, bonds, income-generating real estate, or intellectual property. You should stay at your day job but start buying real assets, not just things that lose value the moment you take them home.
