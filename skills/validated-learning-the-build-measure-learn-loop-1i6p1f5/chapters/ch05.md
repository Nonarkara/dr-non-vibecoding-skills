# ch05 — Vanity Metrics vs. Actionable Metrics

> Understanding the difference between vanity metrics (that look good but don't provide insights) and actionable metrics is critical.

Not all metrics are created equal. Vanity metrics – such as website visits or social media followers – can be misleading because they don’t directly reflect customer behavior or product value. Actionable metrics, on the other hand, provide insights that you can use to make informed decisions and drive your startup forward.
