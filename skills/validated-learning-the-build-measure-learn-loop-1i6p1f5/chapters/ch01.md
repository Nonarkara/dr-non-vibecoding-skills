# ch01 — The Startup Problem

> Most startups fail due to building the wrong thing or failing to adapt quickly. The Lean Startup offers a framework for navigating this uncertainty.

Startups face immense challenges: high failure rates, limited resources, and operating under conditions of extreme uncertainty. Traditional business planning often exacerbates these problems by assuming a lot about the future. The Lean Startup recognizes this inherent risk and provides a structured approach to mitigate it. It’s not about predicting the market; it’s about learning what the market actually wants through rapid experimentation.
