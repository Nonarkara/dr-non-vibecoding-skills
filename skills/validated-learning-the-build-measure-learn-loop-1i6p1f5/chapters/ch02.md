# ch02 — Defining a Startup

> Ries redefines ‘startup’ as an organization dedicated to creating something new under conditions of extreme uncertainty, applicable to any scale.

Eric Ries argues that the definition of a startup extends beyond just large companies. Whether you're working alone in a garage or leading a team within a Fortune 500 company, the core challenge remains the same: navigating uncertainty and discovering a viable path to a sustainable business. The key is a commitment to validated learning.
