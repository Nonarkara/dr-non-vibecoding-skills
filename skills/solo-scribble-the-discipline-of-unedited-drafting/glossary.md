# Glossary

Terms and named ideas from the source, anchored to the chapter that explains them.

| Term | Definition | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **stream of consciousness** | A writing style that captures thoughts as they occur, without filtering or structuring them first. | ch01 |
| **natural pause** | The point where the story itself signals a break, such as after a scene or thematic segment. | ch02 |
| **keyboard commitment** | The habit of keeping your hands on the keyboard until the idea is fully expressed. | ch03 |
| **next‑day revision** | Reviewing and editing a draft after a full day’s break to gain fresh perspective. | ch04 |
| **distraction‑free environment** | A workspace where notifications, tabs, and other interruptions are removed. | ch01 |
| **edit marker** | A simple placeholder like "[edit]" used to note where you intend to revise later. | ch01 |
| **warm‑up typing** | A short, unrelated typing exercise to get the fingers moving before the main draft. | ch03 |
| **writing ritual** | A repeatable set of actions (e.g., tea, breathing) that signals the brain to start drafting. | ch05 |
| **word count log** | A brief record of how many words were written in a session, used for tracking progress. | ch05 |
| **writing partner** | Another writer who checks in on your schedule and provides accountability. | ch05 |
