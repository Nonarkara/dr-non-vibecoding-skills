# ch10 — Health in the Anthropocene

> The impact of environmental destruction and climate change on human wellbeing.

The 'Anthropocene' refers to the current geological age where human activity is the dominant influence on the environment. This has profound implications for health. Industrialization and globalization have driven the exploitation of the environment, leading to air pollution, climate change, and mega-disasters like hurricanes. These environmental threats are often 'syndemic'—they interact with social and biological threats to create complex health crises. Medical anthropology looks at how indigenous communities deal with these changes and how the destruction of the planet is fundamentally a health crisis for all creatures.
