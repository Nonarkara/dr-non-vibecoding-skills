# ch04 — Framing Effects

> Explains how the same information presented in loss or gain terms changes behavior, and gives guidelines for honest framing.

Framing is the way a choice is described. A statement like "90% survive" leads to higher acceptance than "10% die," even though the facts are identical. Loss frames tend to motivate action more strongly than gain frames, especially for health or safety decisions. However, framing must be truthful; deceptive framing crosses into manipulation. Designers should test both frames with representative users and select the one that best aligns with the intended welfare outcome.
