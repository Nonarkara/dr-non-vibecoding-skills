# ch05 — The iNUDGE Checklist

> Provides a step‑by‑step checklist—Incentives, Defaults, Understanding, Mapping, Feedback, Error avoidance, Structure—for evaluating any UI.

Before finalizing a design, run the iNUDGE checklist: 1) Incentives – are the rewards clear and aligned with the goal? 2) Defaults – does the default steer users toward the desired action? 3) Understanding – can users easily grasp the choice and its consequences? 4) Mapping – are the options laid out in a logical, intuitive way? 5) Feedback – does the system tell users the impact of their decision? 6) Error avoidance – are common mistakes prevented or corrected? 7) Structure – are complex choices broken into manageable steps? Each question forces the designer to consider both the Automatic and Reflective Systems.
