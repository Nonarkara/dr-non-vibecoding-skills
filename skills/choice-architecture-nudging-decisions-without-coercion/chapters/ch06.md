# ch06 — SLIM Messaging

> Introduces the SLIM criteria for crafting messages that actually move behavior.

A nudge works best when the message is Salient (stands out), Likeable (positive tone), In‑the‑moment (delivered at the decision point), and Memorable (easy to recall). For example, a pop‑up that says "Save $5 today by switching to paperless billing" combines a clear financial gain, a friendly tone, and appears right before the payment step, making the choice easy to act on. Designers should test each SLIM element and iterate until the message meets all four criteria.
