## Core principles

- A business model describes how an organization creates, delivers, and captures value.
- The Business Model Canvas visualizes nine interdependent building blocks that together form a complete model.
- Value Proposition is the bundle of products and services that solves a customer problem or satisfies a need for a specific segment.
- Customer Segments define the groups of people or organizations a business aims to serve and create value for.
- Channels are the touchpoints through which a company communicates, delivers, and supports its Value Proposition.
- Customer Relationships describe the types of interactions a business establishes with each segment to acquire, retain, and grow revenue.
- Revenue Streams represent the cash generated from each segment, driven by pricing mechanisms and payment types.
- Key Resources, Activities, and Partnerships are the assets, actions, and alliances required to make the model work.
- Cost Structure outlines all expenses incurred to operate the model, distinguishing between cost‑driven and value‑driven designs.
- Patterns such as unbundling, long tail, multi‑sided platforms, FREE, and open business models provide reusable templates for innovation.
