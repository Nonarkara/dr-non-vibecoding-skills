## Core principles

- Incentives are the cornerstone of modern life and the key to solving any riddle.
- Conventional wisdom is often wrong and usually serves the interests of those who spread it.
- Dramatic effects often have distant, subtle, and non-obvious causes.
- Experts use their informational advantage to serve their own agendas rather than their clients.
- Knowing what to measure and how to measure it makes a complicated world simple.
- Morality represents how people want the world to work, while economics shows how it actually does.
- Cheating is a primordial economic act: getting more for less.
- Data can reveal hidden truths that people are unwilling or unable to speak aloud.
