# ch07 — Information Asymmetry and the Expert's Edge

> How experts use their superior knowledge to manipulate and profit from their clients.

An expert is someone who knows more about a subject than the person they are advising. This 'information asymmetry' gives the expert a massive advantage. Real estate agents, for example, often encourage sellers to accept the first decent offer they receive. While the agent says this is in the seller's best interest, the data shows otherwise. When agents sell their own homes, they keep them on the market longer and sell them for significantly more money. For the agent, the extra $10,000 in sale price for a client only results in a small increase in commission, which isn't worth the extra weeks of work. But for the agent's own home, that $10,000 goes straight into their pocket. Experts use their knowledge to create fear or urgency, leading the client to make a choice that benefits the expert. To protect yourself, you must align the expert's incentives with your own or find ways to bridge the information gap, as the internet has done for things like life insurance and used cars.
