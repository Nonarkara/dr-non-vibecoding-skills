# ch01 — The Value Proposition Canvas

> An overview of the two-sided tool used to visualize how value is created for customers.

The Value Proposition Canvas is the central tool of this methodology. It consists of two parts: the Customer Profile and the Value Map. The Customer Profile is used to clarify your understanding of the person you are serving. It breaks down their world into 'Jobs' (what they are trying to get done), 'Pains' (what annoys or stops them), and 'Gains' (the outcomes they want). The Value Map describes your offer. It lists your 'Products and Services' and explains how they act as 'Pain Relievers' and 'Gain Creators.' You achieve 'Fit' when the two sides align—when your products specifically address the most important parts of the customer's profile. This tool moves the conversation from vague features to concrete value. It serves as a shared language for teams, ensuring everyone understands exactly how the business intends to help the customer. Without this alignment, companies risk building 'features' that nobody cares about.
