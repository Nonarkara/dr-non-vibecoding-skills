# ch03 — Designing the Value Map

> Creating the specific features that relieve pain and create gain.

The Value Map is where you design your solution. It starts with a list of 'Products and Services.' These are the items the customer sees in your shop window. However, products don't create value on their own; they only create value in relation to the customer profile. You must explicitly describe your 'Pain Relievers'—how your product eliminates specific things that annoy the customer. Do you save them time? Do you reduce their risk? Then, describe your 'Gain Creators.' These explain how you produce the outcomes the customer expects or dreams of. A great Value Map does not try to relieve every pain or create every gain. It makes choices. It focuses on a few high-value areas and addresses them better than any competitor. This clarity prevents 'feature creep' and ensures that every part of the product has a clear reason for existing. If a feature doesn't map to a specific job, pain, or gain, it is likely waste.
