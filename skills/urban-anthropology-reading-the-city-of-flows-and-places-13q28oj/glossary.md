# Glossary

Terms and named ideas from the source, anchored to the chapter that explains them.

| Term | Definition | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Flow** | The movement of water, people, capital, or information through urban channels. | ch01 |
| **Space of Flows** | A logic of social organization based on electronic networks rather than physical proximity. | ch03 |
| **Space of Places** | The physical, social, and cultural characteristics of a specific, fixed location. | ch03 |
| **Urban Metabolism** | The process by which cities consume natural resources and produce social outcomes and waste. | ch10 |
| **Commodity Circuit** | The global path a product takes from raw material to urban market and eventual disposal. | ch04 |
| **Diaspora** | A population scattered from their homeland who maintain strong cultural and economic ties across borders. | ch07 |
| **Gemeinschaft** | A type of social bond based on intimate, traditional, and often rural community ties. | ch06 |
| **Gesellschaft** | A type of social bond based on impersonal, modern, and often urban associations. | ch06 |
| **Growth Coalition** | A group of elites who manage finance and laws to promote urban development and capital return. | ch04 |
| **Ideoscape** | The global flow of political and social ideas centered on urban nodes. | ch01 |
| **Mediascape** | The distribution of electronic capabilities to produce and spread information and images. | ch01 |
| **Ethnoscape** | The shifting landscape of persons who constitute the moving world in which we live. | ch05 |
| **Technoscape** | The global configuration of technology and the speed at which it moves across boundaries. | ch03 |
| **Financescape** | The rapid and complex movement of global capital through currency markets and stock exchanges. | ch04 |
| **Flâneur** | An urban observer who wanders through the city to analyze its social life and power dynamics. | ch09 |
| **Dual City** | A city polarized between high-tech elites and the service workers who support them. | ch03 |
| **Transnationalism** | Social processes and networks that extend beyond and operate across national borders. | ch07 |
| **Imaginary** | A shared set of ideas or images about how people should live together in a society. | ch06 |
| **Infrastructure** | The physical systems, such as pipes and wires, that enable urban life and reflect social power. | ch02 |
| **Exclusion** | The systematic denial of access to urban resources, space, or citizenship rights. | ch08 |
