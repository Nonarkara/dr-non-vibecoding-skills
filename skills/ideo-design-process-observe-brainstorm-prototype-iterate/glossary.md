# Glossary

Terms and named ideas from the source, anchored to the chapter that explains them.

| Term | Definition | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **observation** | Watching people in their natural environment to see what they actually do, not what they say they do. | ch01 |
| **bug list** | A running note of things that frustrate, confuse, or hinder users during observation. | ch01 |
| **being left‑handed** | Setting aside one’s own expertise to see the world from the user’s point of view, especially when the user differs from the designer. | ch01 |
| **hot team** | A small, multidisciplinary group given a clear goal and a short deadline to create momentum and focus. | ch06 |
| **prototype** | A low‑fidelity artifact built to answer a specific design question, not to prove a solution. | ch04 |
| **iteration** | A cycle of building, testing with users, learning, and refining a prototype or concept. | ch05 |
| **constraint‑driven design** | Treating limits on time, budget, or materials as inputs that focus and fuel creativity. | ch07 |
| **brainstorming rules** | The four IDEO guidelines: defer judgment, go for quantity, build on others, encourage wild ideas. | ch03 |
| **cross‑functional collision** | An unplanned interaction between people of different disciplines sparked by a shared prototype or workspace. | ch06 |
| **human factors** | The study of how people interact with products and systems, focusing on behavior, abilities, and limitations. | ch01 |
| **latent need** | A user need that is not openly expressed but can be inferred from observed behavior or workarounds. | ch01 |
| **rapid prototyping** | Building quick, cheap prototypes to learn and iterate rather than to create a final product. | ch04 |
| **design sprint** | A five‑day, time‑boxed process that adapts observation, brainstorming, prototyping, and iteration for fast concept testing. | ch10 |
| **empathy** | Deep understanding of a user’s feelings, motivations, and context gained through observation and immersion. | ch01 |
| **feedback loop** | The process of gathering user reactions to a prototype and using those insights to inform the next iteration. | ch05 |
| **design brief** | A concise statement of the problem, target user, constraints, and goals that guides the design effort. | ch02 |
| **innovation culture** | Shared habits and values that make observation, experimentation, and learning from failure routine. | ch09 |
| **prototype wall** | A physical space where prototypes are displayed for anyone to interact with and comment on. | ch06 |
| **design for manufacturability** | Adapting a concept so it can be produced reliably, safely, and at target cost. | ch08 |
| **design drift** | The gradual loss of the original innovative idea as practical concerns accumulate during development. | ch08 |
