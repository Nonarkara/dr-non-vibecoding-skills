# ch07 — Constraints as Fuel for Creativity

> Use time, budget, and material limits to focus ideation and drive innovative solutions. This chapter shows how to reframe constraints as design parameters that spark creativity.

Instead of treating constraints as limitations, IDEO teams explicitly list them and then ask how each constraint can inspire a solution. Time limits force the team to prioritize the most critical questions and avoid over‑engineering. Budget limits encourage the use of inexpensive, readily available materials, which can lead to unexpected material combinations. Material limits push designers to think about alternative processes or geometries that achieve the same function with less. For example, when designing a low‑cost medical device for emerging markets, the team embraced a tight budget by using off‑the‑shelf plastic tubing and simple snap‑fit connectors, which kept costs down while maintaining reliability. The process begins with a constraint‑storming session: the team writes each constraint on a card, then brainstorms ways to turn that constraint into a design feature or advantage. By viewing constraints as creative prompts, the team avoids the paralysis that comes from an open‑ended brief and instead generates focused, feasible ideas that are more likely to succeed in the real world.
