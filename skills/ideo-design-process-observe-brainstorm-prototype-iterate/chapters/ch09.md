# ch09 — Building a Culture of Innovation

> Foster an environment where observation, experimentation, and learning from failure are everyday practices. This chapter covers rituals, workspace design, and mindset shifts that sustain innovation.

A repeatable innovation process depends on a culture that supports its core behaviors. IDEO encourages teams to keep bug lists, celebrate intelligent failures, and regularly practice observation exercises such as walking through a user’s environment and noting surprises. Workspaces are designed to be flexible, with movable walls, writable surfaces, and areas for displaying prototypes, which invite spontaneous collaboration. Storytelling is used to share lessons learned: after each project, teams present what they observed, what they tried, what worked, and what they would do differently, turning experience into organizational knowledge. Leaders model the mindset by asking ‘What did we learn?’ instead of ‘Who is to blame?’ when something does not work. This creates psychological safety, allowing team members to propose wild ideas without fear of ridicule. Over time, these practices embed observation, prototyping, and iteration into the way work gets done, making innovation a habit rather than a one‑off event.
