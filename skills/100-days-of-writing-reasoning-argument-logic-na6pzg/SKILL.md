## When to load this skill

A toolkit for intellectual honesty and clear thinking. This skill teaches how to dismantle social stigmas, use constraints to find clarity in writing, and navigate existential loneliness through pragmatic action and the recognition of personal bias.

## Core principles

- Question traditions and norms that lack a clear productive logic.
- Use strict constraints in expression to force complex thoughts into a clear rhythm.
- Recognize that state-driven stigmas are often tools of biopower used to control populations.
- Acknowledge that all observation is reflexive; when you describe a field, you describe yourself.
- Distinguish between the anxiety of being unwanted and the freedom of being alone.
- Accept weakness as a shared human condition rather than a flaw to be hidden.
- Use pragmatic busyness to prevent the mind from wandering into non-productive despair.
