# Cheatsheet

Imperative decision rules from the source, grouped by category. Each is the kind of sentence you would write on an index card.

## process

- If a tradition lacks a productive logic, you are not obligated to follow it.
- If a task feels overwhelming, focus on the immediate pragmatic step in front of you.

## general

- Anxiety is often a tool used by the state to make you a 'stable' citizen.

## writing

- To find the truth in a complex thought, write it in the simplest possible English.
- Your description of the world is always a description of yourself.

## design

- Don't mistake the 'uncanny' feeling of familiarity for a genuine connection.

## life

- Loneliness is the only time you are truly free from social judgment.
- When despair hits, stay busy with pragmatic tasks until the mind stabilizes.
- Weakness is the one thing all humans share; don't be afraid to show it.
- The best way to avoid being taken for granted is to not reply to messages instantly.

## argument

- If you are going to conform, do it honestly; don't pretend to be a rebel while obeying.
- A society without fathers exists; therefore, the nuclear family is a choice, not a law of nature.
