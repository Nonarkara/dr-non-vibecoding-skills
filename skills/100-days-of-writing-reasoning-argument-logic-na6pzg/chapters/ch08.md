# ch08 — Structure vs. Agency

> Navigating the dualism of human nature and the trap of 'insecurity loneliness.'

Emile Durkheim described the 'dualism of human nature' as the struggle between our individual capacity to act (agency) and the social structures we live in. A common trap is 'insecurity loneliness,' where a person tries to appear independent and unique while actually being deeply submissive to social authority and credentials. For example, someone might walk fast to show they are 'high-paced' but stop religiously at a red light even when no cars are coming. This person is stuck between what they think they want (to be seen as special) and what they really want (to conform and be safe). To avoid this, you must be honest about your conformity. If you choose to follow a rule, do it because you find comfort in the morality of it, not because you are pretending to be something you aren't. Authenticity requires accepting your weaknesses and being honest about where you fit into the structure.
