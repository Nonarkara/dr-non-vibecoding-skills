# Glossary

Terms and named ideas from the source, anchored to the chapter that explains them.

| Term | Definition | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Biopower** | A technique used by states to achieve the subjugation of bodies and the control of populations. | ch02 |
| **Productive Logic** | The rational, functional purpose behind a tradition or action. | ch01 |
| **Leftover Women** | A derogatory state-sponsored label for urban professional women in China unmarried past age 27. | ch02 |
| **Zomia** | Geographical areas in Southeast Asia historically beyond the control of lowland governments. | ch03 |
| **Matrilineal** | A system of kinship where descent and inheritance are traced through the female line. | ch03 |
| **Lignée** | A line of blood-related descendants from a common ancestor. | ch03 |
| **Uncanny** | The strange attraction to something that feels familiar due to past impressions or resemblances. | ch05 |
| **Reflexivity** | The act of acknowledging that the observer's perspective is part of the thing being observed. | ch05 |
| **Existential Loneliness** | An unavoidable human condition of being alone with one's own subjective truth. | ch06 |
| **Bad Faith** | The act of lying to oneself or conforming to social roles to avoid the pain of freedom. | ch08 |
| **Agency** | The individual capacity to act, think, and make rational choices independently. | ch08 |
| **Structure** | The social frameworks, rules, and authorities that govern individual behavior. | ch08 |
| **Dualism of Human Nature** | Durkheim's concept of the tension between individual agency and social structure. | ch08 |
| **Pragmatism** | An approach that evaluates theories or beliefs based on the success of their practical application. | ch07 |
| **Jean-Paul Sartre** | Existentialist philosopher who argued that existence precedes essence and humans are born without fixed purpose. | ch06 |
| **Arthur Schopenhauer** | Pessimistic philosopher who viewed solitude as the only state of true freedom. | ch06 |
| **Haruki Murakami** | Novelist who used linguistic constraints to develop a simple, rhythmic writing style. | ch04 |
| **Dr. Cai Hua** | Anthropologist who studied the Na society and their lack of traditional marriage. | ch03 |
