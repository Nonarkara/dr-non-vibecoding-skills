# ch06 — Starting Points: Push vs. Pull

> How to begin the design process from technology or market needs.

There are two main ways to start designing a value proposition. 'Technology Push' starts with an invention or innovation you already have. You are essentially a 'solution in search of a problem.' In this case, you must explore different customer segments to find who has jobs, pains, and gains that your technology can address. 'Market Pull' starts with a manifest customer problem. You observe a group of people struggling with a task and then design a solution to help them. Both are valid, but they require different mindsets. Push requires you to be an explorer, testing your tech in various contexts. Pull requires you to be an anthropologist, deeply observing customer behavior to find unsatisfied needs. Regardless of where you start, you must end at the same place: a proven fit between your value map and a customer profile.
