# ch10 — Six Ways to Innovate

> Strategies for evolving your value proposition based on the profile.

Once you have a customer profile, there are six ways to trigger innovation. 1. Address more jobs: Solve a complete set of related tasks (like the iPhone combining a phone, music player, and internet device). 2. Switch to a more important job: Help the customer do something they care about more. 3. Go beyond functional jobs: Fulfill social or emotional needs. 4. Help more customers: Make a complex or expensive solution accessible to a larger group. 5. Get a job done incrementally better: Make small, meaningful improvements to an existing solution. 6. Get a job done radically better: Create a new market by dramatically outperforming old ways of working. Innovation isn't always about new technology; it's often about finding a better way to map your offer to the customer's existing life.
