# ch08 — Customer Insights and Research

> Techniques for gathering evidence and understanding the customer.

To design a great value proposition, you must get out of the building. Use the 'Data Detective' technique to look at existing research and secondary data. Use the 'Journalist' technique to talk to potential customers. When interviewing, don't ask 'Would you buy this?' Instead, ask about their past behavior and current struggles. Use the 'Anthropologist' technique to observe customers in their natural environment. Watch how they actually use products, not how they say they use them. You might discover 'workarounds'—hacks customers have created to solve a problem—which are great indicators of a gap in the market. The goal of this research is not to sell, but to listen. You are looking for evidence that validates or invalidates the sticky notes on your Customer Profile.
