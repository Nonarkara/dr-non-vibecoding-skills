# ch09 — B2B and Stakeholder Mapping

> Designing for complex organizations with multiple decision-makers.

In Business-to-Business (B2B) sales, the 'customer' is rarely one person. It is a group of stakeholders, each with their own jobs, pains, and gains. You must map a profile for each role. The 'Influencers' provide opinions that matter. The 'Recommenders' evaluate the options. The 'Economic Buyers' control the budget and care about efficiency. The 'Decision Makers' have the final say. The 'End Users' are the ones who will actually use the product. Crucially, you must identify the 'Saboteurs'—those who might lose power or status if your solution is adopted and will try to block it. A value proposition that delights the end user but ignores the economic buyer's need for cost-cutting will fail. You must achieve fit for the most important stakeholders in the chain for the sale to happen.
