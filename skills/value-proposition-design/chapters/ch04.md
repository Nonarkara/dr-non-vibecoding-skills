# ch04 — The Three Stages of Fit

> Understanding the progression from an idea to a profitable business.

Fit happens in three distinct stages. The first is 'Problem-Solution Fit.' This occurs on paper when you identify jobs, pains, and gains that customers care about and design a value proposition that addresses them. At this stage, you have no proof—only a good plan. The second stage is 'Product-Market Fit.' This happens in the wild. You have evidence that your products, pain relievers, and gain creators are actually creating value and gaining traction. You will likely find that many of your early assumptions were wrong, requiring you to pivot or refine your design. The third stage is 'Business Model Fit.' A great product is useless if it costs more to deliver than it earns. This stage is reached when you have evidence that your value proposition can be embedded in a profitable, scalable business model. You must constantly move between these stages, testing and iterating until you have a solution that customers want and that makes money.
