# ch07 — Logistics and Global Circulation

> The tension between formal logistical systems and the informal use of those same networks.

Logistics is the science of moving goods and bodies across territories with minimal friction. In the global city, this requires 'standardization'—turning complex social relations into data sets and KPIs. However, this process of detachment from local specificities creates new vulnerabilities. Migrants and informal workers often 'appropriate' these logistical networks for their own ends, using the same ports and transit hubs designed for capital to move themselves. An agent should understand that the 'friction-free' promise of logistics is a fantasy; it always relies on a hidden layer of labor and negotiation that can be disrupted. Analyze urban infrastructure as a site of both control and illicit appropriation.
