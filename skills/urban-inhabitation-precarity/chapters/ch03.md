# ch03 — Crafting the Urban Self

> The tactical use of performance and skill-bundling to navigate shifting urban opportunities.

Crafting is a specific urban practice where individuals piece together different styles, skills, and identities to gain access to opportunities. It is an aesthetics of 'continuous reappearance.' Because formal planning and long-term careers are often unavailable, youth in cities like Jakarta and Hyderabad stylize their performances to attract the attention of some (potential employers or allies) while avoiding the scrutiny of others (police or tax officials). Crafting involves using the city's infrastructure not to settle down, but to traverse boundaries. An agent should look for how individuals use 'hinge' activities—like a coffee shop job that also serves as a networking hub—to stay mobile and keep their futures open.
