# ch05 — The Thinking Body

> How physical exercise like biking acts as a mental decompression chamber.

Some of the best ideas come when you are not trying to think. Physical activities like biking or running take you out of your immediate concerns. When you focus on the road or the rhythm of your breath, your mind stops working in the present and moves to a different space. This decompression allows your 'true self' to emerge. By removing the pressure of being an 'expert' or a 'writer,' you give your brain the breathing space it needs to solve complex problems. Use your exercise time to listen to podcasts or audiobooks, letting the movement of your body fuel the movement of your ideas.
