# ch06 — Writing Degree Zero

> The importance of writing for yourself to avoid the trap of audience expectation.

When you write knowing that someone will read it, you unconsciously censor yourself. You worry about how you will be perceived, which does an injustice to the ideas in your head. 'Writing Degree Zero' is the practice of writing for the sake of writing. Think of your writing as a private field note or a diary. By removing the ghost of the reader from the room, you allow yourself to be more honest and more radical. This lack of anxiety toward reception is where original thinking begins. You can decide later what to share, but the act of creation should be for you alone.
