# ch08 — Weaving the Abstract

> How to connect mundane daily observations to high-level philosophical arguments.

Great non-fiction moves between the specific and the universal. You might start with a simple observation, like the rise of Starbucks in China, and use it to explore the loss of private space or the rise of capitalism. This is the art of weaving. Use your personal life—your fatigue, your bike rides, your conversations—as the 'data' for your philosophical inquiries. By grounding abstract ideas in concrete, everyday experiences, you make complex thoughts accessible and relatable to the reader. Never let a theory stand alone; always tie it back to a moment you lived.
