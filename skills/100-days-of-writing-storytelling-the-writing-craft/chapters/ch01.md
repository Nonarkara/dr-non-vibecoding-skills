# ch01 — Writing to Learn

> Understanding writing as a tool for personal growth and cognitive processing rather than just a way to talk to others.

Writing is something you must do alone. While others can help you edit, the original idea only comes when you are by yourself. Many people view writing as a way to communicate, but its primary value is as a way to learn. There is a gap between the moment an idea appears in your head and the moment your fingers put it into words. In that gap, you are forced to think more carefully. By writing, you become a mediator for your own immature thoughts, allowing yourself to think twice before reaching a conclusion. Treat writing as a friend who offers silent, critical help in processing the world.
