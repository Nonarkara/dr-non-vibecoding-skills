# ch02 — The Power of the First Story

> How to bypass writer's block by starting with your most vivid memories instead of your notes.

When you sit down to write, do not start with your data, your books, or your external references. Instead, reach for the single most powerful story you have in your head. If you were telling a friend about your day over a glass of wine, what is the first thing you would say? Start there. Your memory is the best filter for what actually matters. By letting the story lead the writing, you capture the qualitative nuances of a situation that formal notes often miss. Once the story is on the page, you can bring in theories and references to support it.
