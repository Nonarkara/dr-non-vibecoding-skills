# Cheatsheet

Imperative decision rules from the source, grouped by category. Each is the kind of sentence you would write on an index card.

## process

- If you don't know what to write, write to find out.
- Look at the floor of the tunnel (daily steps), not just the walls (final goal).
- Reflection is the difference between a path and a random walk.

## writing

- Write until you physically drop to capture a true stream of consciousness.
- Censor the audience, not your own ideas.
- A powerful story is a better anchor than a perfect theory.
- Treat writing as a friend who helps you think twice.
- The best writing happens in the gap between the idea and the fingers.

## general

- Never follow a tradition that you cannot defend with reason.

## life

- Use exercise to take the pressure of being an 'expert' off your shoulders.
