# Glossary

Terms and named ideas from the source, anchored to the chapter that explains them.

| Term | Definition | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Writing Degree Zero** | The act of writing for the sake of writing, without a conscious sense of an audience. | ch06 |
| **Internal Empiricism** | A focus on one's own perceptions and reason rather than societal expectations. | ch07 |
| **Codifying** | The process of turning raw daily experiences into structured knowledge through writing. | ch03 |
| **Self-imposed Immaturity** | The state of following traditions or others' ideas without exercising one's own reason. | ch07 |
| **Ethnographic Moment** | A powerful, vivid encounter from real life that serves as the anchor for a story. | ch02 |
| **Stream of Consciousness** | A writing style that captures a continuous flow of thought without interruption. | ch04 |
| **Non-possessive Love** | A relationship based on mutual intellectual respect rather than ownership or control. | ch07 |
| **The Mediator** | The role writing plays in helping a person process immature or raw thoughts. | ch01 |
| **Closing-off** | The mental state of becoming so absorbed in a task that you lose awareness of the outside world. | ch03 |
| **Informant** | A person from whom a researcher or writer gathers cultural or personal information. | ch02 |
