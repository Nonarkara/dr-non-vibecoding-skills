# ch08 — Ritual Condensation

> Turning abstract ideas into material objects.

Humans have a limited capacity for holding complex abstract ideas in their minds. To manage this, we project these ideas onto the external world, turning 'mentifacts' into material objects. This is called ritual condensation. For example, the abstract idea of 'divine potency' is hard to grasp, so a culture might represent it with a physical object like a cross or a carved stone. These objects become 'sacred' because they are infected by the aura of the concept they represent. This allows people to perform physical actions—like bowing or offering food—to interact with an abstract idea. This process is the basis of all religious ritual and iconography.
