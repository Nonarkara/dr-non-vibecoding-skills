# Glossary

Terms and named ideas from the source, anchored to the chapter that explains them.

| Term | Definition | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **visceral level** | The fast, preconscious system that judges safety and desirability from sensory input. | ch01 |
| **behavioral level** | The system that evaluates how well a product supports goal‑directed action and the pleasure of use. | ch02 |
| **reflective level** | The conscious system that thinks about self‑image, memories, and meaning of a product. | ch03 |
| **affect** | The feeling component of emotion that signals whether something is good or bad, safe or dangerous. | ch04 |
| **cognition** | The thinking component that assigns meaning and understands how the world works. | ch04 |
| **positive affect** | A relaxed, happy state that broadens attention and encourages creativity. | ch04 |
| **negative affect** | A tense, anxious state that narrows focus on details and threats. | ch04 |
| **bottom‑up processing** | Thinking driven by sensory input and perceptual data. | ch04 |
| **top‑down processing** | Thinking driven by goals, expectations, and higher‑level ideas. | ch04 |
| **pattern matching** | The visceral level’s method of recognizing familiar configurations without reasoning. | ch01 |
| **self‑image** | The mental picture a person has of who they are, which products can reinforce or challenge. | ch07 |
| **product personality** | The set of consistent traits (tone, appearance, behavior) that make a product feel like a particular kind of character. | ch07 |
| **kitsch** | Objects dismissed as tasteless but valued for the memories and sentiments they evoke. | ch06 |
| **flow** | A state of deep immersion where action and awareness merge, often facilitated by well‑designed objects. | ch06 |
| **affective response** | The emotional reaction (positive or negative) that a design triggers in a user. | ch04 |
| **affordance** | A property of an object that suggests how it can be used, guiding correct behavior. | ch02 |
| **feedback** | Immediate information that lets users know the result of their action. | ch02 |
| **mental model** | The internal representation users have of how a system works, based on experience. | ch02 |
| **personalization** | Features that let users imprint their own identity or story onto a product. | ch06 |
| **cultural probe** | A research method that explores symbolic meanings, values, and practices within a target culture. | ch08 |
