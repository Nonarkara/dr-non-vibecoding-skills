# ch08 — From Concept to Commercialization

> Take a refined prototype and move toward implementation while preserving the creative intent. This chapter covers engineering handoff, design for manufacturability, and maintaining the innovation spirit through scale.

Once a prototype has been validated through multiple iterations, the focus shifts to turning it into a product that can be made and sold at scale. IDEO works closely with engineering and manufacturing partners to ensure that the design can be produced reliably, safely, and within cost targets. This involves creating detailed specifications, selecting appropriate materials, and designing for assembly and serviceability. Throughout this phase, the team guards against ‘design drift’—the gradual loss of the original innovative idea as practical concerns accumulate. To prevent this, they keep the original prototype and insight statements visible, regularly refer back to the user needs that sparked the project, and involve user‑testing even during late‑stage validation. For example, the development of the Neat Squeeze toothpaste tube involved constant reference to the original observation that kids gripped brushes with their fists, ensuring the final tube’s shape remained easy to hold despite changes in material thickness and sealing mechanisms. The goal is to launch a product that feels true to the initial insight while meeting all business and regulatory requirements.
