# ch10 — Applying the SUCCESs Checklist

> Provides a step‑by‑step method for taking any idea, finding its core, and then making it Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, and Story‑based to maximize stickiness.

To make an idea stick, follow two phases. Phase 1: Find the core. Ask the Commander’s Intent questions—what is the single most important thing you must communicate? Strip away everything else until only that essence remains. Phase 2: Apply the SUCCESs checklist to the core. First, ensure it is Simple: is it short and focused, or does it contain unnecessary details? Second, add Unexpectedness: what question or gap will make the audience lean in? Third, make it Concrete: replace abstractions with sensory details and specific examples. Fourth, build Credibility: decide whether to use an authority, an anti‑authority, vivid details, or the Sinatra Test. Fifth, evoke Emotion: tie the idea to the audience’s self‑interest, identity, or emotions like resentment. Sixth, wrap it in a Story: choose a challenge, connection, or creativity plot that lets the audience mentally simulate acting on the idea. After drafting, test the message with an unfamiliar audience—if they grasp the core and can recall it later, the idea is sticky. Iterate as needed, using the checklist as a diagnostic tool each time.
