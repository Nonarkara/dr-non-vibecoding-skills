# ch02 — Prerequisites & Deliverables

> Details the minimum inputs and expected outputs for each method, ensuring the AI runs them with full rigor.

### What Every Method Needs
1. **Participants** – Who is involved? Some methods need strangers (e.g., Extreme User Interviews), others need internal staff (e.g., Guided Tours).
2. **Materials** – Physical or digital artifacts such as cameras, sticky notes, or software tools.
3. **Time** – Estimated duration; most cards note a typical session length.
4. **Context** – The environment where the method makes sense (e.g., a hospital for Fly on the Wall).

### Typical Deliverables
- **Artifacts** – Photo logs, affinity clusters, prototype models, or annotated maps.
- **Insights** – Themes, pain points, or opportunity statements.
- **Documentation** – Consent forms, data logs, or iteration notes.

### Example: Affinity Diagram
- **Prereqs**: Raw data (quotes, observations), participants for clustering, sticky notes.
- **Deliverable**: A visual map of themes with headings that can be used for synthesis.

### Example: Experience Prototype
- **Prereqs**: Materials to build a mock‑up, a test scenario, participants willing to interact.
- **Deliverable**: A prototype plus a list of observed issues and user reactions.

The AI should verify that all prerequisites are satisfied before invoking a method and record the deliverable for downstream phases.
