# ch07 — Cross‑Cultural & Extreme‑User Adaptations

> Describes how to adjust methods for global contexts or for users at the edges of the target market.

### Why Adapt?
Designs often fail when they ignore cultural nuances or extreme use cases. Methods like **Foreign Correspondents** and **Extreme User Interviews** surface hidden constraints.

### Adaptation Steps
1. **Identify Target Variation** – Is the need cultural, geographic, or ability‑based?
2. **Select Base Method** – Choose the method that aligns with the variation (e.g., Cultural Probes for culture, Extreme User Interviews for edge cases).
3. **Adjust Logistics** – Translate materials, arrange local facilitators, or modify consent forms.
4. **Validate Relevance** – Pilot the adapted method with a small sample.
5. **Integrate Findings** – Feed insights back into the main synthesis.

### Example
Instead of a standard Survey for a global product, the AI can run **Foreign Correspondents** to collect anecdotes from local teams, then synthesize those into a **Conceptual Landscape** that reflects regional differences.
