# ch05 — Participant Ethics Checklist

> Provides a concise checklist to guarantee respectful and legal treatment of participants.

### Core Ethical Rules
1. **Introduce Yourself** – State name, role, and purpose.
2. **Seek Informed Consent** – Explain what will be recorded and why.
3. **Compensate Fairly** – Offer payment or a token of appreciation.
4. **Explain Data Use** – Tell participants how their input will influence design.
5. **Allow Opt‑Out** – Participants can stop at any time without penalty.
6. **Maintain Confidentiality** – Store data securely and anonymize when sharing.
7. **Create a Safe Atmosphere** – Keep the setting relaxed and non‑judgmental.

### Quick Checklist for the AI
- [ ] Have I introduced the study?
- [ ] Is consent documented?
- [ ] Is compensation arranged?
- [ ] Have I explained data usage?
- [ ] Is the participant comfortable to continue?
- [ ] Are privacy safeguards in place?

If any item is unchecked, the AI must pause the method and address the gap before proceeding.
