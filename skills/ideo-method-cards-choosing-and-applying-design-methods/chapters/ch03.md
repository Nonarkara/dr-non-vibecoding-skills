# ch03 — Method Substitution Rules

> Guides the AI on how to replace unavailable methods with suitable alternatives while preserving intent.

### When Substitution Is Needed
- **Missing Participant Type** – No access to extreme users.
- **Resource Constraints** – Lack of materials for a high‑fidelity prototype.
- **Time Pressure** – Need a faster insight.

### Substitution Principles
1. **Match Goal** – Identify the core purpose (e.g., uncover hidden motivations) and find another method that targets the same goal.
2. **Maintain Rigor** – The substitute must still collect comparable data quality.
3. **Document Rationale** – Record why the swap was made for future audit.

### Common Substitutions
| Original Method | Substitute | Reason |
|-----------------|------------|--------|
| Extreme User Interviews | Structured Interviews with a broader sample | Extreme users unavailable but still need deep insights |
| Paper Prototyping | Digital Wireframe in a tool like Figma | No physical materials, but visual fidelity is sufficient |
| Fly on the Wall | Video Observation | Direct presence not possible, but video captures behavior |
| Cultural Probes | Online Diary Study | Physical probes not feasible, but digital logs work |

The AI should follow the three‑step recipe: identify goal → list viable alternatives → pick the one with highest data fidelity.
