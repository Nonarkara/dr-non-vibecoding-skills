# ch06 — Overkill Detection & Scaling Down

> Helps the AI recognize when a method is too heavyweight for the problem and suggests lighter alternatives.

### Signs of Overkill
- **Large Group Size** for a tiny design tweak.
- **High‑fidelity Prototypes** when only concept validation is needed.
- **Lengthy Ethnographic Studies** for a simple UI change.

### Scaling‑Down Strategies
1. **Reduce Participants** – From 12 to 3‑5 for quick feedback.
2. **Simplify Materials** – Use paper instead of 3D printing.
3. **Shorten Duration** – Limit interviews to 15 minutes.
4. **Swap Method** – Replace Cultural Probes with a short Survey.

### Decision Rule
*If the expected insight can be captured in less than 2 hours of work, choose the lighter method.*

The AI should run a quick cost‑benefit check before launching any method and automatically suggest a scaled‑down version when the cost outweighs the benefit.
