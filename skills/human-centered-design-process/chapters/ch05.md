# ch05 — Recruiting the Right People

> Explains how to build a balanced participant pool using extremes, main‑streams, and demographic filters.

Effective discovery requires talking to both extreme users and mainstream users. Begin by listing the dimensions that matter—age, gender, income, location, and role—and then plot participants across those spectra. Use the "Extremes and Mainstreams" checklist to ensure at least one person from each extreme (e.g., a single‑person household and a large extended family) and a representative sample of typical users. Consider cultural sensitivities: match interviewers to participants when gender dynamics could affect openness. Track recruitment in a spreadsheet, noting contact method, consent status, and scheduled date. This chapter gives the AI a template for building inclusive, insight‑rich participant lists.
