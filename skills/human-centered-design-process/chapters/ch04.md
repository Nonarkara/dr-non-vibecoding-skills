# ch04 — Interview Methods

> Details individual, group, and expert interview techniques, including preparation, execution, and note‑taking.

Interviews are the core tool for gathering empathy. An individual interview should involve no more than three team members—interviewer, note‑taker, and photographer—to avoid crowding the participant. Prepare a flexible guide that starts with broad life questions, then narrows to challenge‑specific probes. Record verbatim quotes and observe body language. Group interviews gather diverse perspectives quickly; assemble 7‑10 participants representing different demographics, ask one facilitator to lead while others capture responses, and use strategies to draw out quieter voices. Expert interviews add systems‑level context; select a mix of viewpoints, keep the agenda light, and record insights for later triangulation. This chapter equips the AI to schedule, script, and synthesize interview data.
