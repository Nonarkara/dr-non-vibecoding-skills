# ch08 — Iterative Learning Loop

> Explains how to embed continuous learning, measurement, and refinement throughout the HCD process.

Iteration is not a single phase but a loop that runs from inspiration through implementation. After each test, extract three actionable insights: what worked, what failed, and what is unknown. Prioritize insights by impact and ease of implementation, then plan the next prototype or interview. Record metrics (e.g., task success rate, satisfaction score) to track progress over time. Use a simple Kanban board to visualize ideas in "To Test," "Testing," and "Validated" columns. The loop ends when the solution meets the minimum lovable criteria and the team has confidence in scalability. This chapter gives the AI a structured learning cycle to apply after every activity.
