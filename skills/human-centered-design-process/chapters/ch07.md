# ch07 — Prototyping & Testing

> Guides rapid paper prototyping, usability testing, and feedback loops to move ideas toward a minimum lovable product.

Prototyping turns ideas into tangible artifacts that users can interact with. Start with low‑fi paper or cardboard models; keep them simple enough to change within minutes. Conduct a usability test by inviting a participant to complete a task while the team observes and records pain points. Ask open‑ended follow‑up questions: "What would make this easier?" Capture both verbal feedback and body language. Iterate immediately: adjust the prototype, retest with a new participant, and repeat until the solution feels both functional and desirable. The goal is a minimum lovable version—functional enough to solve the core need and appealing enough to want to use. This chapter provides a test script and iteration checklist for the AI.
