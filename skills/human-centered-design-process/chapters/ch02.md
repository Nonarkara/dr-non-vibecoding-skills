# ch02 — Inspiration Phase Overview

> Outlines the purpose of the Inspiration phase and lists the key activities that move a team from ignorance to insight.

The Inspiration phase is the discovery stage where the team gathers raw knowledge about the people, context, and constraints. It begins with building a project plan that defines timeline, budget, and required skills. Then the team conducts secondary research to understand the broader landscape, followed by immersive fieldwork—interviews, home tours, and observation—to hear stories directly from users. Throughout, the team records quotes, photos, and notes, building a repository of insights that will later fuel synthesis. The phase ends with a clear, human‑centered framing of the design challenge, ready for ideation. Loading this chapter equips the AI to structure discovery work, allocate resources, and keep the focus on people.
