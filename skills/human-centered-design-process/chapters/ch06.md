# ch06 — Ideation Tools

> Covers How‑Might‑We framing, Conversation Starters, Card Sorts, and Analogous Inspiration for generating ideas.

After the Insight pool is built, the team moves to ideation. Convert each insight into a "How might we…" question to keep the focus on people and possibilities. Use Conversation Starter cards—wild, absurd, or simple prompts—to spark reactions and uncover hidden needs. Conduct a Card Sort by giving participants a deck of words or images and asking them to rank what matters most; analyze the order for priority signals. For fresh angles, perform Analogous Inspiration: list the behaviors you want to study, then find unrelated settings (e.g., a coffee shop for queuing) that exhibit similar patterns, and observe them. This chapter equips the AI to generate prompts, run card sorts, and harvest analogies.
