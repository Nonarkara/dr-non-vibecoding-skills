# Glossary

Terms and named ideas from the source, anchored to the chapter that explains them.

| Term | Definition | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Empathy** | Understanding people’s lives by listening to their words and observing their actions. | ch01 |
| **Optimism** | Belief that a solution exists and that constraints can spark creativity. | ch01 |
| **Ambiguity** | The state of not knowing the answer, used as a space for idea generation. | ch01 |
| **Iteration** | Repeating design, test, and refine cycles until the solution works. | ch01 |
| **Minimum Lovable** | A product that not only works but also feels desirable enough to adopt. | ch01 |
| **Design Challenge** | A concise, impact‑focused question that guides ideation. | ch03 |
| **Interview** | One‑on‑one conversation that captures a participant’s story in their own words. | ch04 |
| **Group Interview** | Facilitated discussion with multiple participants to surface diverse viewpoints. | ch04 |
| **Expert Interview** | Conversation with a specialist to add systems‑level context to user insights. | ch04 |
| **Extreme Users** | People at the far ends of a user spectrum whose needs reveal hidden opportunities. | ch05 |
| **Mainstream Users** | Typical users who represent the majority of the target audience. | ch05 |
| **How‑Might‑We** | A framing question that turns an insight into an open design prompt. | ch06 |
| **Conversation Starter** | A provocative idea or sketch used to spark reactions from participants. | ch06 |
| **Card Sort** | A ranking exercise where participants order cards to reveal priorities. | ch06 |
| **Analogous Inspiration** | Looking at unrelated settings for ideas that can be adapted to the design problem. | ch06 |
| **Paper Prototype** | A low‑fi, quickly made model used to test concepts with users. | ch07 |
| **Usability Test** | A session where a participant attempts a task while the team observes and records feedback. | ch07 |
| **Kanban Board** | A visual workflow tool that tracks ideas from "To Test" to "Validated". | ch08 |
| **Decision Tree** | A flowchart that helps decide whether a problem is a design issue or another type. | ch09 |
| **Pilot Rollout** | A small‑scale launch used to validate a prototype before full scaling. | ch10 |
| **Stakeholder Alignment** | Ensuring all partners agree on goals, metrics, and responsibilities. | ch10 |
