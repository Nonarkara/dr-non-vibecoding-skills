# ch05 — The Collaborator

> The role that brings people together and dissolves the boundaries between departments.

The Collaborator is the glue that holds a diverse team together. They focus on the 'we' rather than the 'I.' In an innovation context, the Collaborator's job is to ensure that the different 'faces' are talking to each other. They are excellent at running brainstorms and ensuring that every voice is heard. They understand that the best ideas often happen at the intersection of different disciplines. They work to remove the 'silo' mentality where engineers only talk to engineers and designers only talk to designers. The Collaborator is often the one who organizes the 'war room' or the shared workspace where the team's progress is visible to everyone. They are the coaches who help the team stay motivated during the difficult middle phases of a project.
