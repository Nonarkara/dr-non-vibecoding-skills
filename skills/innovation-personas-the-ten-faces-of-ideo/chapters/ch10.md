# ch10 — The Storyteller

> The role that builds internal and external support for an idea through narrative.

The Storyteller knows that facts and figures don't move people; stories do. They are responsible for creating the 'mythology' of a project. They use videos, anecdotes, and metaphors to explain why an innovation matters. A Storyteller can take a dry engineering breakthrough and turn it into a compelling vision of the future. They are essential for getting buy-in from executives and for launching a product to the public. They don't just describe what a product does; they describe how it changes the user's life. The Storyteller ensures that the 'why' of the project is never lost in the 'how.' They are the ones who make the innovation memorable and shareable.
