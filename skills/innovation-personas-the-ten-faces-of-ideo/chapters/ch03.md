# ch03 — The Cross-Pollinator

> The role that connects disparate ideas from different industries to create something new.

Cross-Pollinators are 'T-shaped' people: they have deep expertise in one area but a broad interest in many others. They act as the bees of the organization, carrying ideas from one field and planting them in another. They look for metaphors and analogies. For example, a Cross-Pollinator might look at how a pit crew works in Formula One to improve the efficiency of a hospital operating room. They are avid readers of magazines and books outside their specialty and frequent places like newsstands with thousands of titles to spark new connections. They understand that most 'new' ideas are actually old ideas applied in a new context. To be a Cross-Pollinator, you must be a collector of stories, technologies, and business models, constantly asking, 'How could we use this here?'
