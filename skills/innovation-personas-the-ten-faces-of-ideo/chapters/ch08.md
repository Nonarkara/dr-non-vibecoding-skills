# ch08 — The Set Designer

> The role that uses the physical workspace to influence behavior and spark creativity.

The Set Designer understands that the environment dictates the action. If you want people to collaborate, you don't put them in cubicles; you give them shared tables and movable furniture. The Set Designer treats the office as a prototype that can be changed daily. They use 'project rooms' where the walls are covered in sketches and notes, making the team's thinking visible. They know that a change in scenery can lead to a change in mindset. They might move a team into a van, a retail store, or a temporary 'pop-up' space to get them closer to the problem they are solving. The Set Designer's job is to create a space that says, 'It's okay to try things here,' and 'This is a place where ideas are born.'
