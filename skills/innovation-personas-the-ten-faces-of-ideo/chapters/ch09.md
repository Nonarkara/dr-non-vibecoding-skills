# ch09 — The Caretaker

> The role that ensures the existing service remains excellent while new innovations are developed.

The Caretaker is the most underrated role. While the other faces are looking for the 'new,' the Caretaker is looking after the 'now.' They ensure that the core business doesn't fail while the team is experimenting with the future. In a service context, the Caretaker is the person who anticipates the customer's needs before they even ask. They are the flight attendant who remembers your name or the tech support person who follows up to make sure the problem is truly solved. They provide the stability that allows the rest of the team to take risks. Without the Caretaker, innovation can feel like a distraction from the company's primary mission. They turn a one-time transaction into a long-term relationship.
