# ch03 — The Build-Measure-Learn Loop

> This chapter introduces the core framework of the Lean Startup – a continuous cycle of building, measuring, and learning.

The Lean Startup’s central concept is the build-measure-learn loop. This iterative process involves creating a Minimum Viable Product (MVP), gathering data on its performance, and using that data to refine your understanding and make informed decisions. It's not about building the perfect product; it’s about building something quickly and learning from it.
