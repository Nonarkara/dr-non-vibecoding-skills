# ch08 — Interpreting Rituals as Texts

> Treats rituals like literary texts, where each element can be read for symbolic and social significance.

Rituals are performative texts that encode cultural values, power relations, and collective memory. By parsing a ritual into its constituent actions—opening, climax, closure—the interpreter can map each step to a symbolic referent. For AI, this means building a hierarchical model of ritual structure, tagging each segment with its emic explanation, and linking it to the broader cultural themes identified in earlier chapters. The result is a layered reading that respects both the choreography and the underlying meaning.
