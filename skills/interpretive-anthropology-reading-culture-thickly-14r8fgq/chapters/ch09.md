# ch09 — Contextualizing Interpretation

> Shows how to situate any cultural description within its historical, geographic, and social context to avoid misreading.

Every cultural act is embedded in a specific context—historical events, economic conditions, power dynamics, and local histories. Geertz argues that meaning cannot be extracted without attending to these layers. The AI must therefore attach metadata to each thick description: time, place, participants, and relevant background facts. When comparing across cases, the system should first align contexts before looking for similarities. This contextual grounding prevents the misapplication of one culture’s logic to another’s distinct situation.
