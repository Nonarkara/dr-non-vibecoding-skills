# Cheatsheet

Imperative decision rules from the source, grouped by category. Each is the kind of sentence you would write on an index card.

## process

- Treat societies as variables, not things.
- Look for the ratio between parents, not just the presence of a bond.
- Watch for 'butterfly collecting' in your own analysis.
- Use topology to find constants in changing systems.

## design

- Ignore biological facts; focus on social dogmas.
- Identify who provides the 'bone' and who provides the 'flesh.'
- Remember that structural patterns can survive radical shape changes.
- Treat 'mother's husband' as a distinct category from 'father.'

## argument

- Use algebra to strip away value-loaded language.

## general

- Distinguish between involuntary fate and intentional magic.
- Check if siblings through one parent are considered unrelated.

## product

- Map supernatural threats to political hierarchies.
- Look for 'perpetual affinity' in political structures.

## writing

- Avoid universal definitions of 'marriage' or 'family.'
- Don't let English kinship terms bias your observations.
