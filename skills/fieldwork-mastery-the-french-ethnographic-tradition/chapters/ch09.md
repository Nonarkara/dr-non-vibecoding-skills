# ch09 — The Logic of Hierarchy and Values

> How to identify the underlying value systems that drive social action.

Every society is driven by a set of core values that dictate how people interact. In some cultures, the primary value is individualism, while in others, it is a 'holistic' view where the individual is subordinate to the group. Understanding these values is more important than simply documenting symbols. For instance, a system based on 'purity' and 'impurity' creates a hierarchy that governs every aspect of social life, from who you can eat with to what jobs you can perform. To uncover these values, you must look at social action in context. You must identify the 'hierarchical oppositions' that the community uses to organize its world. This allows you to see the structure of the society as a whole, rather than just a collection of random customs.
