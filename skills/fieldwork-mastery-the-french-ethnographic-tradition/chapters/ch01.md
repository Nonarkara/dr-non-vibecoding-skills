# ch01 — The Art of Defamiliarization

> How to strip away common sense to see the world with fresh eyes.

To become a true observer, you must practice the discipline of 'keeping your eyes open.' This involves a deliberate mental shift where you treat your own culture or a familiar environment as if it were an unexplored land. You must assume you know nothing about the language, the buildings, or the people. By ignoring what you think you know, you can document the small details that others overlook. This process of defamiliarization turns the ordinary into a series of questions. Instead of seeing a 'square,' you see a specific triangular layout that dictates how people walk and interact. This method requires you to be physically present and to test the impact of the environment on your own body and emotions. It is the first step in moving from a passive resident to an active researcher.
