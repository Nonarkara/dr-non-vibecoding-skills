# ch04 — Physical Movement as Idea Generator

> Describes how activities like biking create mental space for creative breakthroughs and can be deliberately used in design work.

The author recounts that many of his best ideas arrived while biking, because the rhythmic motion frees the mind from immediate task pressure. This aligns with research on "incubation"—a period where the brain works on a problem subconsciously while you engage in a low‑cognitive‑load activity. By scheduling a short ride or walk after a design sprint, you give the brain a chance to recombine elements in new ways. The practice also reduces stress, improving focus when you return to the desk. For product teams, a "bike break" can be institutionalized: after a sprint demo, the team steps outside for ten minutes of light activity, then reconvenes to discuss insights that surfaced during the break. This habit turns a simple physical routine into a systematic source of fresh perspectives.
