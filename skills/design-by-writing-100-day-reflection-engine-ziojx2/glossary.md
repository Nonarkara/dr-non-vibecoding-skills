# Glossary

Terms and named ideas from the source, anchored to the chapter that explains them.

| Term | Definition | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **writing** | The act of turning internal thoughts into external text that can be examined and edited. | ch01 |
| **stream of consciousness** | A free‑flow writing technique that captures raw impressions without self‑censorship. | ch02 |
| **reflection** | A short, structured review of what was created to surface hidden assumptions. | ch03 |
| **iteration loop** | A cycle of prototype, test, reflect, and refine. | ch03 |
| **incubation** | Mental processing that occurs while engaged in low‑cognitive‑load activities like biking. | ch04 |
| **tradition audit** | A systematic check that treats established practices as hypotheses to be validated. | ch05 |
| **non‑possessive empathy** | Observing users without trying to own or control their behavior. | ch06 |
| **closing‑in** | A cognitive trap where deep focus blinds peripheral awareness. | ch07 |
| **incrementalism** | Making small, testable changes rather than large, risky redesigns. | ch08 |
| **failure mode** | A predictable way a design process can go wrong, such as over‑focus or possessive bias. | ch07 |
| **habit loop** | Cue‑routine‑reward pattern that reinforces a behavior. | ch01 |
| **user story** | A concise narrative describing a user's need or experience. | ch02 |
| **prototype** | A low‑fidelity version of a design used to test an idea quickly. | ch08 |
| **A/B test** | Comparing two variants to see which performs better on a metric. | ch08 |
| **ethnographic moment** | A vivid, memorable observation from field work that can drive design insight. | ch02 |
