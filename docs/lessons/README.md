# Lessons

Post-session lesson docs, in the shape of [`templates/lesson.md.template`](../../templates/lesson.md.template)
and under the discipline of [`skills/lesson-residue`](../../skills/lesson-residue/SKILL.md).

This repository is the meta layer, so most of its durable reasoning is already in
[`playbooks/`](../../playbooks/) — a playbook is a lesson that survived long enough
to be generalised. What lands here first is narrower: a specific session, a specific
surprise, with the date and the verbatim ask attached.

Relay legs write here too. Anything a leg wants the *next* leg to understand goes in
[`docs/relay/RELAY.md`](../relay/RELAY.md) as a fact or an open question; anything a
leg wants to explain — the reasoning, the alternatives, the argument for a choice —
goes here, where the next leg can read it *after* forming its own view rather than
before. That ordering is the whole point of
[`skills/agent-relay`](../../skills/agent-relay/SKILL.md).
